Inspiration

Why specify MStone recycled stone

This engineered surface suits projects that want to reduce their impact without giving up everyday comfort. According to MStone Surfaces, its carbon footprint can be cut by up to 40%, and the absence of quartz makes it a different material from standard quartz.

In use, it is easy to live with: non-porous, it needs no treatment, and it is stated to be non-yellowing, an important point for light colours. It withstands 180 °C of dry heat; beyond that, a trivet is essential.

Its pattern runs through the full thickness. In practice, edges, sink or hob cut-outs and any small scratches keep the same look as the surface, without revealing a different layer. We machine it to your drawings, in 20 mm, then check every piece before dispatch.

The six MStone recycled stone colours

The six colours divide into bright lights, sandy tones and deep shades.

  • Apollon: a greyish off-white with soft clouds and fine taupe flecks, for a light, natural kitchen.
  • Orion: an ivory white with delicate diagonal beige-grey veins, more elegant.
  • Athena: a greige scattered with fine particles, very easy to pair with wood.
  • Hyperion: a deep sandy beige leaning towards camel, warm and tone on tone.
  • Athos: a uniform mid-grey with a very fine grain, for a sober, contemporary look.
  • Helios: an anthracite close to slate, crossed by fine white veins.

Two finishes are available, matt or polished, in 20 mm. Matt brings out the raw mineral look, polished highlights the flecks. State the colour and finish on your drawings.

Your questions

What does non-yellowing mean for a worktop?

Some resin-bound surfaces can take on a slightly yellow tint over time under the effect of light. MStone recycled stone is stated by its manufacturer to be non-yellowing: its light colours, such as Apollon or Orion, are designed to keep their shade. It is an important criterion for a very bright kitchen or a white, and one worth highlighting to your clients.

What upkeep should be described for MStone recycled stone?

It is one of the simplest to look after: soapy water and a soft sponge every day, with no sealing at all. The user wipes up liquids promptly, avoids harsh strippers and abrasive pads, and always places very hot pans on a trivet, the stated resistance being 180 °C of dry heat. A chopping board protects the surface from blades.
