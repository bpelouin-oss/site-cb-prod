Marble, for an exceptional worktop or basin

No surface matches the depth of a marble worktop: its veins tell a different story on every slab. A living material, softer than granite, it should be chosen with your eyes open. We help you find the right marble and finish, then cut it to measure in Mafra.

Worktops in Marble

The ultimate symbol of refinement. Its unique veining makes it a living, incomparable material. Ideal for statement basins, high-end kitchens and bathrooms. Mohs hardness 3–4; sealing recommended.

Marble — points to note

  • Mohs hardness 3–4
  • Sensitive to acids — sealing recommended
  • Ideal for bathrooms and decorative pieces
  • Use in kitchens with care
  • 20 or 30 mm

Our projects

Why choose marble for your worktop

People choose marble for the emotion it brings. A Statuario vanity top, a Noir Marquina table or a Calacatta Borghini island transform an entire room. Marble develops a patina over time, and many of our clients love precisely that sense of a living material.

It must be accepted with its limits: its Mohs hardness of 3 to 4 makes it more prone to scratches, and the acids in lemon, vinegar or wine can dull the polish. In a kitchen, it suits people who are happy to wipe up quickly and use a trivet, or an island that sees less action than the prep area.

In a bathroom, on a console or a table, it shows its full potential with very few constraints. Sealing is still advisable in every case.

White, black or coloured: choosing your marble

White marbles remain the most requested. Statuario and Blanc de Carrare offer a luminous background with fine veins, while Calacatta Borghini has more dramatic grey veining, ideal for a worktop that should be the focal point of the room.

Dark marbles create striking contrasts: Noir Marquina with its bright white veins, or Portoro with its golden veins, look superb in a bathroom or as a splashback. For a touch of colour, Verde Alpi brings a deep green, and Rose Portugal, a salmon-pink marble with white veins, echoes Portugal's stone tradition.

On pale marbles, a honed matt finish hides the light marks left by acids better than a high polish, which remains the most brilliant.

Your questions

Does a marble worktop scratch easily?

More than granite or quartzite, yes. With a Mohs hardness of 3 to 4, marble can be marked by hard objects, rough pottery or blades. Always use a chopping board and felt pads under decorative items. The good news is that natural stone can be repolished: a professional can soften scratches and signs of wear.

Is marble a good choice for a bathroom vanity top?

It is one of its favourite uses. In a bathroom it faces fewer knocks and acidic liquids than in a kitchen, and its beauty comes fully into its own. A few precautions are enough: up-to-date sealing, wiping away toothpaste and cosmetics, and gentle cleaning products rather than acidic descalers. We cut the top with all the basin and tap cut-outs.
