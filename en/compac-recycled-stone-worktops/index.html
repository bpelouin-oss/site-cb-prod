Inspiration

Why choose Compac recycled stone for your worktop

Compac recycled stone is an eco-designed mineral composite that draws on the brand's expertise in technological quartz. It has low porosity, needs no sealing and stands up well to both scratches and knocks.

Its major asset for professionals is consistency. From one batch to the next, the colours stay coherent, making it possible to fit out several homes, a hotel or an office space with a uniform result. It also suits projects pursuing HQE, BREEAM or LEED.

The three thicknesses, 12, 20 and 30 mm, cover every use from splashback to island top, and two finishes, Glacé and Polished, are offered. Architects and kitchen designers benefit from our trade prices.

Choosing your Compac recycled stone colour

The Volcano series makes up most of the range. Its colours share the same signature, a fleck of fine recycled chips, in a wide variety of tones.

  • For a light kitchen: Volcano Cloud, a cloudy white, Volcano Pearl or Volcano Light.
  • For a neutral feel: Volcano Grey, Volcano Dim, a muted grey, or Volcano Fog.
  • For bolder tones: Volcano Cool, a slightly bluish grey, the warmer Volcano Warm, or Volcano Dark, a deep anthracite scattered with grey and white chips.

Set apart, Unique Marquina captures the spirit of the famous Nero Marquina, a deep black with crisp white veins, for anyone wanting a marble effect with a recycled approach. The Volcano fleck has the practical advantage of hiding crumbs and small marks well.

Your questions

Do Compac recycled stone colours vary between batches?

Very little: consistent colour from batch to batch is one of the strengths of this range, unlike natural stone. That is what makes it suitable for housing schemes, hotels or projects carried out in several phases. For a large project, we organise supply so as to keep any differences between pieces to an absolute minimum.

Which thickness of Compac recycled stone should I choose?

12 mm suits splashbacks, wall cladding, furniture and bathroom tops, for a very slim line. 20 mm is the standard for kitchen worktops. 30 mm gives a more substantial look, popular on islands or counters. When measuring up, our surveyors confirm the thickness based on the layout and planned overhangs.
