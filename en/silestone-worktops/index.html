We turn Silestone® slabs into made-to-measure worktops in our Mafra factory, complete with sink and hob cut-outs and a matching splashback.

Inspiration

Why choose Silestone® for your worktop

Silestone® ticks the boxes for a family kitchen: a non-porous surface, no sealing required, NSF certification and good resistance to scratches, stains and knocks. Cosentino's HybriQ® technology also aims for better environmental performance in manufacturing.

Its strength also lies in the variety of finishes. Polished brings out the depth of the colours, Suede offers a matt finish that is soft to the touch, and Volcano a rougher texture with a raw stone look. Thicknesses of 20 and 30 mm let you play with the worktop's presence.

As with any quartz, the only real precaution concerns intense heat: a trivet is a must for pans coming off the hob or out of the oven. The manufacturer's warranty varies according to the range chosen.

Choosing your Silestone® colour

Among the Silestone® colours we offer, a few families stand out.

  • Marble effects such as Eternal Calacatta Gold or Eternal Statuario, for a bright, elegant kitchen without the constraints of real marble.
  • Whites such as White Platinum, with its crystalline glints, or the more uniform Iconic White, for a light-filled kitchen.
  • Blacks and anthracites such as Negro Tebas or Charcoal Soapstone, which add character to a wood or lacquer kitchen.
  • More unexpected shades such as Calypso, a turquoise, or Poblenou, to get off the beaten track.

Not every finish is offered for every colour: the catalogue below shows which. A sample remains the best way to judge the result in your own light.

Your questions

Polished, Suede or Volcano: which Silestone® finish should I choose?

Polished is the easiest to clean and intensifies the colours. Suede, matt and soft to the touch, gives a more natural look and reduces reflections, but shows greasy marks more, which come off with a suitable cleaner. Volcano, with its texture, recalls raw stone and suits projects with a bold style. Not every colour is available in all three finishes.

Silestone® or Dekton®: which should I choose?

Both are made by Cosentino, but they are different materials. Silestone® is a quartz: non-porous, consistent and very pleasant in a family kitchen, with one precaution regarding intense heat. Dekton® is an ultra-compact ceramic that copes better with heat, UV and outdoor use. If you often put down scorching pans or are planning a summer kitchen, Dekton® has the edge.
