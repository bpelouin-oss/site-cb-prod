Outdoor worktops built to last in the open air

An outdoor kitchen worktop lives outside all year round: sun, rain, temperature swings and barbecue grease. For landscapers, kitchen studios and interior contractors, we manufacture these worktops in ceramic and granite, the surfaces suited to this use, from your drawings, then deliver them from Mafra.

Ceramic, the go-to material outdoors

Ultra-compact ceramic is the solution we recommend most often outdoors. Dekton®, made by sintering natural minerals using Cosentino's TSP process, has near-zero water absorption and high resistance to UV, heat, scratches and stains. MStone ceramic, non-porous and needing no treatment, is unaffected by sunlight, and Infinity even extends to facades.

For your clients, this means:

  • colours that stay stable in the sun;
  • a hot dish set down without fear of marking;
  • grease and wine stains that come off with soapy water.

Frost resistance depends on the reference and the exposure. We check the manufacturer's recommendations with you, which is essential for an outdoor kitchen facing harsh winters.

Granite, a natural stone made for outdoors

A very hard igneous rock, granite copes well with heat, scratches and knocks, valuable qualities around a barbecue. Every slab is unique, from deep Nero Assoluto Zimbabwe to lighter Silver Cloud, and we select the blocks from our international suppliers.

Outdoors, the finish matters as much as the stone. Flamed granite, produced by exposure to very high heat, has a rough, less slippery surface that works well on worktops left out in the rain. Leather finish, softer to the touch, hides marks well.

Natural granite remains slightly porous: sealing is recommended to limit grease stains, and Sensa treated versions make upkeep simpler. A very dark stone also gets hotter in full sun than a light one, a detail worth pointing out to clients with a south-facing terrace.

Preparing your outdoor kitchen file

An outdoor kitchen is designed like a real kitchen: cooking zone, sink, prep space and often a serving counter. We make bespoke cut-outs for the built-in barbecue, plancha or sink, and manufacture side cladding to create a single, solid unit.

A few points to plan for on your drawings:

  • the references of built-in appliances, for exact cut-outs;
  • a slight fall or drip edge, so rain does not sit on the worktop;
  • a matt or textured finish, less slippery and more discreet in direct light;
  • in full sun, ceramic rather than quartz or resin-bound surfaces, which are more sensitive to UV.

Send us your dimensioned drawings, for example in DXF, DWG or PDF: we reply within 48 working hours, then check and deliver every piece.

Your questions

What is the best material for an outdoor worktop?

Ultra-compact ceramic such as Dekton® is the most versatile choice outdoors: near-zero water absorption and resistance to UV, heat and stains. Granite is an excellent natural alternative, especially with a flamed or leather finish. For frost, we check the manufacturer's recommendations with you for the chosen reference and the project's region.

Can quartz be specified for an outdoor kitchen?

It is not recommended in full sun. Quartz contains resins that can react to UV over time and change colour. Under a fully covered shelter the situation is different, but ceramic remains the safer option. Describe the expected exposure in your enquiry and we will advise you case by case.

What care advice should I pass on to clients?

Ceramic just needs cleaning with soapy water, even after a barbecue, and no treatment is required. Granite is cleaned the same way but gains from regular sealing against grease stains. In both cases, it is best not to leave leaves and standing water on the surface, and to cover the kitchen if it is not used in winter.
