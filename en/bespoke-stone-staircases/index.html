Stone treads, risers and landings, made to your drawings

As a bespoke stone staircase manufacturer, CB Prod fabricates the treads, risers, landings and skirtings of your projects in its Mafra factory, in marble, granite, quartzite or engineered materials. Architects, interior contractors and building firms send us their drawings; we manufacture, inspect and deliver, while setting in place stays with your teams.

The staircase elements we make

A stone staircase brings together several pieces that must match perfectly, in both dimensions and appearance. We make to measure, from your drawings:

  • treads, straight or winding, with their nosings;
  • risers, matching the treads or in contrast;
  • landings, in one or several pieces;
  • skirtings and string cladding, cut to follow the pitch;
  • thresholds and transition pieces towards adjoining floors.

For a harmonious result, we prepare the cutting plan so that the elements of a single staircase come from a consistent batch of slabs, taking the direction of the veining into account on natural stone.

Each element is CNC cut, which keeps the treads of a flight consistent, then finished by hand on the edges and nosings, where the stone is most seen and most touched.

Choosing the staircase material and finish

A staircase sees constant foot traffic, so the choice of material and finish matters as much as the look.

Granite and quartzite, both very hard, stand up well to wear and suit high-traffic areas. Marble offers unmatched elegance for an indoor staircase, provided you accept that it will develop a patina over time. Some ceramics and quartz surfaces can also be considered, depending on the ranges and thicknesses available.

The finish has a direct effect on comfort and safety. A polished finish brings out the veining but is more slippery; matt or textured finishes, where the range offers them, give better grip, especially outdoors or near an entrance.

Tell us the intended use, indoor or outdoor, residential or open to the public: we point you towards suitable materials and finishes, and send you the manufacturers' technical documentation.

What to send us, and delivery

We manufacture from the information you give us, with on-site surveying remaining your responsibility or that of your partners. For a staircase, please send us:

  • dimensioned drawings of the staircase and each element, with the number of steps, their going and rise;
  • any technical files available, for example DXF, DWG or dimensioned PDF;
  • the material, thickness and finish required;
  • the profile of the nosings and visible edges;
  • additional elements: landings, skirtings, thresholds;
  • the delivery address and access constraints.

Each piece is inspected for dimensions and finish, then securely packed and labelled according to your drawings so it is easy to identify on site. We deliver to France, Portugal and internationally. You receive a quote within 48 working hours once we have studied your file.

Your questions

Do you measure the staircase on site?

No. CB Prod does not take measurements on site or set pieces in place. Surveying the staircase, often tricky on an existing structure or a concrete flight, remains your responsibility or that of your partners. We check your drawings for consistency before manufacturing and flag any inconsistency in rise or going.

Which material should I recommend for an outdoor staircase?

Granite is often the first choice: hard and little affected by frost and weather, it accepts textured finishes that improve grip. Some ceramics designed for outdoor use are also possible, depending on the range. Avoid polished finishes outdoors. Describe the exposure and use to us and we will point you towards suitable references and their documentation.

Will the treads of one staircase look consistent?

We prepare the cutting plan so that the elements of a single staircase come from a consistent batch of slabs and follow the direction of the veining. Natural stone nevertheless keeps variations from one slab to the next, which are part of its character. If uniformity is essential to your client, a factory visit by appointment lets you approve the slabs before cutting.
