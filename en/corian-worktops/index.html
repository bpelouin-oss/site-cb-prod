Inspiration

Why choose Corian® for your worktop

Corian® is a homogeneous solid surface: its colour and composition are the same throughout its thickness. Pieces are joined with virtually invisible seams, so a basin or sink can be integrated with no lip or visible silicone. When heated, the material can be thermoformed into curves, thinned edges or shaped counters.

Another unique advantage: scratches or minor burns can be polished out. It is non-porous, NSF/ANSI 51 certified, and some colours even allow backlighting.

You do need to know its limits. In our selection guide, Corian® is less resistant to scratches and heat than stone or ceramic: a chopping board and a trivet are essential. That is the price of its freedom of form.

Corian® uses and colours: making the right choice

Corian® excels in bathrooms: one-piece vanity tops, moulded basins with no joins and continuous wall cladding. In kitchens, it suits projects where an integrated sink and easy cleaning come first, as well as free-form counters. It is also widely used in shops and reception areas.

With more than 100 colours, the choice is vast. Glacier White, a pure white, remains the benchmark for a crisp, bright look. Cameo White, a pearly white, and Antique White, a cream, add more warmth. For stone-effect lovers, Carrara Lino or Calacatta Greige recall marble, while Deep Nocturne or Lava Rock play on depth.

Thicknesses of 6, 12 and 19 mm, in high gloss or satin matt, cover wall applications as well as worktops.

Your questions

How do you repair a scratch on a Corian® worktop?

Because the colour runs through the full thickness, a surface scratch can be sanded and repolished without changing the look of the worktop. On a matt finish, a lightly abrasive sponge is often enough for micro-scratches. For a deeper mark or a burn, it is best to call in a professional, who will restore the surface locally. This is one of Corian®'s great advantages.

Corian® or quartz for a bathroom vanity top?

If you want an integrated basin with no joins at all and rounded shapes, Corian® is unbeatable. If you prefer a harder surface that scratches less and tolerates a hot hair dryer or straighteners being put down, quartz or ceramic will be more relaxed, with a countertop or undermount basin. Both are non-porous and easy to clean.
