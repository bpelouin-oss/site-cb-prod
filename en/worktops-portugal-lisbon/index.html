A worktop factory in Mafra, near Lisbon

With family craftsmanship handed down over more than three generations, CB Prod makes bespoke worktops at its factory in Igreja Nova, on the Estrada Nacional N°9 at kilometre 29. Being based in the Lisbon area keeps us close to our Portuguese clients, both homeowners and professionals.

We craft every one of our material families here: granite, marble and quartzite, Silestone®, Compac, Stone Italiana and MStone quartz, large-format ceramics, Corian®, Minéralis and recycled stone. Each piece is CNC-cut to a tenth of a millimetre, then finished by hand.

Kitchens, bathrooms, tables, bar counters and fireplace surrounds: the projects we carry out for the region leave these workshops under the eye of the team that prepared your quote.

Visiting the factory and choosing your slab

Living near Lisbon has one big advantage: you can come and see the materials in person before you decide. A marble or quartzite slab is best chosen on site, because its veining varies from one slab to the next and a photo never shows everything.

We welcome you at Igreja Nova from Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:30 pm, by appointment only. You can see the slabs available, compare polished, matt and textured finishes, and handle samples of quartz, ceramic and Minéralis.

To save time, bring your kitchen or bathroom plan and a few photos. We can then talk specifically about cut-outs, thickness and splashbacks, and prepare your free quote straight away.

Fitting your worktop in Portugal

In Portugal, as in France, our own teams come to your home. Once you accept the quote, a surveyor takes the exact measurements: lengths, angles, hob openings, the position of the sink and taps. Production starts from that survey.

On fitting day, our installers adjust and secure the worktop, seal the joints and check every cut-out. Before leaving, they explain how to look after your material day to day.

If you are renovating with a kitchen company or a builder, we can also supply the worktop alone, ready to fit. Professionals in the region benefit from preferential terms as well. For any information, call +351 219 672 216 or email direction@cbproducao.com.

Your questions

Where is your factory near Lisbon?

Our factory and head office are at Estrada Nacional N°9, Km 29, Igreja Nova, 2640-339 Mafra, about 40 km from Lisbon. We are open Monday to Friday from 8:00 am to 5:30 pm, and visits are by appointment only, so that we can prepare the slabs and samples suited to your project.

Do you fit worktops in Lisbon?

Yes. Our teams fit worktops across Portugal, including Lisbon and the surrounding area. Survey, manufacture in Mafra and installation are all handled by CB Prod, with one contact from quote to fitting. Give the site address in your request and an expert will reply with a proposal within 48 working hours.

Can I choose my stone slab at the factory?

Yes, and it is actually recommended for natural stone such as marble, granite or quartzite, where every slab is unique. During your appointment at Igreja Nova you can see the slabs available and pick out the part of the veining you would like on your worktop, island or splashback.
