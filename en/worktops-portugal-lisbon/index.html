Manufacturing and delivery

Our Mafra factory, around forty kilometres from Lisbon

Are you a kitchen studio, stonemason, architect or interior contractor in Lisbon or elsewhere in Portugal? Our factory is in Igreja Nova, in the municipality of Mafra. There we make your bespoke worktops and stone pieces from your drawings, then deliver them within Portugal or hand them over at the factory, by appointment.

A worktop factory in Mafra, near Lisbon

With family craftsmanship handed down over more than three generations, CB Prod manufactures to measure in its Igreja Nova factory, Estrada Nacional N°9, kilometre 29. Being based in the Lisbon region puts us close to our Portuguese trade customers.

There we fabricate all our material families: granite, marble and quartzite, Silestone®, Compac, Stone Italiana or MStone quartz, large-format ceramics, Corian®, Minéralis and recycled stone. Each piece is CNC cut from your drawings, then finished by hand and inspected before it leaves the workshop.

Kitchen worktops, vanity tops, cladding, staircases, tables or bar counters: the pieces we make for your projects in the region leave these workshops under the eye of the team that studied your file.

Delivery in Portugal or collection from the factory

Once inspected, your pieces are securely packed and delivered to the address you give us: your workshop, your depot or your client's site, in Lisbon or anywhere else in Portugal. Tell us in your enquiry about any access constraints and your preferred timing.

If you would rather arrange transport yourself, collection from the Igreja Nova factory is possible by appointment, Monday to Friday between 8.00am and 5.30pm. We prepare the pieces and their protection in advance so that loading is quick.

As in France, CB Prod does not take measurements on site or set pieces in place. These services remain your responsibility or that of your partners, which leaves you in control of the schedule and of the relationship with your client.

Visiting the factory and choosing slabs

Working near Lisbon has an advantage: you can come and see the materials in person before approving a project. A marble or quartzite slab is best chosen on site, because its veining varies from one slab to the next and a photo never shows everything.

We welcome you to Igreja Nova from Monday to Friday, 8.00am to 5.30pm, by appointment only, on your own or with your client. You can see the slabs available, compare polished, matt or textured finishes, and handle samples of quartz, ceramic or Minéralis.

To save time, bring your dimensioned drawings. We can then talk in concrete terms about the cutting plan, thicknesses, edges and cut-outs, and prepare your quote straight away. For any information, call +351 219 672 216 or email direction@cbproducao.com.

Your questions

Where is your factory near Lisbon?

Our factory and head office are at Estrada Nacional N°9, Km 29, Igreja Nova, 2640-339 Mafra, around forty kilometres from Lisbon. We are open Monday to Friday from 8.00am to 5.30pm. Visits and collections are by appointment only, so that we can prepare the relevant slabs, samples or pieces.

Can we collect the pieces from the factory?

Yes. If you prefer to arrange transport, you can collect your pieces from Igreja Nova by appointment on weekdays. We prepare them in advance, inspected and protected for loading. Plan a vehicle suited to the size and weight of the pieces: we give you this information before collection to avoid any unpleasant surprises.

Can we choose the stone slab at the factory?

Yes, and it is recommended for natural stone such as marble, granite or quartzite, where every slab is unique. During a visit to Igreja Nova by appointment, on your own or with your client, you see the slabs available and can pick out the part of the veining you want on the worktop, island or splashback.
