Kitchen splashbacks and wall panels, made to measure

A kitchen splashback protects the wall from splashes, but it is also one of the most visible surfaces in the room. For kitchen studios and interior contractors, we manufacture splashbacks and wall cladding in large panels, without the joints of tiling, matched to the worktop or in contrast, from your drawings.

Which materials for a kitchen splashback?

A splashback does not take the same punishment as a worktop: nothing is chopped on it, but it does catch splashes and, behind the hob, heat. The materials we manufacture most often:

  • ceramic (Dekton®, MStone, Infinity), non-porous and heat tolerant, ideal behind a hob; MStone's 12 mm is perfect on walls and Dekton® comes in 8 mm;
  • The Size ceramic, available in 6 mm, for a very slim, light splashback;
  • MStone quartz and Minéralis, uniform and easy to clean;
  • marble and quartzite, for a striking splashback, with marble needing to be sealed.

The choice is usually made alongside the worktop: the same material for continuity, or a deliberate contrast to give the kitchen more depth.

Matching, contrasting or full-height

A matching splashback, cut from the same material as the worktop, gives the kitchen a very clean read. With a veined stone, the effect is even stronger when the veining runs on from worktop to wall: show this on your drawings and we will plan the cutting of the pieces from the same slab. Several of our projects take this approach, such as a Calacatta Vagli Rosato marble splashback above a white worktop, or cream Breccia Sarda marble for both worktop and splashback.

Contrast works just as well: a plain worktop with a heavily veined splashback, or the reverse. For height, there are several options: a simple upstand of a few centimetres, a splashback between worktop and wall units, or full-height cladding that turns the wall into a stone panel, like a wall panel in Black Tempest quartzite, black with gold veining.

Cut-outs, checks and delivery

A large-panel splashback cannot be adjusted on site the way tiles can: it has to be right from the factory. That is why we work solely from your dimensioned drawings, which should show the real wall dimensions, any out-of-plumb areas and the position of every socket, switch or extractor outlet. Site surveys remain with you or your partners.

In our Mafra workshop, panels are CNC cut, openings included, then visible edges are finished by hand. Every piece is checked before dispatch, then securely packed, which is essential for thin, large-format panels. We deliver the splashback with the worktop or separately, across France, Portugal and internationally.

Your questions

What thickness should a kitchen splashback be?

A splashback does not need the thickness of a worktop. Depending on the range, we manufacture in 6 to 12 mm ceramic, light and ideal on walls, or in the same material as the worktop in 12 or 20 mm for seamless continuity. The choice depends on the wall, the fixing method your teams plan to use and the effect you want.

Which material works behind a hob?

Choose a material that copes with heat and splashes. Ceramic is the safest choice: it withstands heat, is unaffected by grease and cleans without special products. Granite also works well. With quartz or marble, make sure the safety distances recommended by the hob manufacturer are respected.

Can you make a splashback on its own, without a worktop?

Yes, a splashback on its own is entirely possible, for example in a renovation where the existing worktop is kept. The material then needs to suit what is already there. Send us a dimensioned drawing of the wall with its openings and a few photos of the kitchen: we will send you a quote within 48 working hours.
