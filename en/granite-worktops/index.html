Inspiration

Why choose granite for your worktop

Granite is the stone for kitchens that are used every day. In our selection guide it scores top marks for resistance to scratches and heat: you can knead dough, set down a casserole or slide a frying pan across it without a second thought.

Its real constraint is stain care. Granite is still a porous stone, more or less depending on the variety, and regular sealing is advisable, especially on pale colours. If that puts you off, the version treated in the factory by Cosentino, Sensa granite, solves the problem.

We work it in 20 or 30 mm, and the finish changes its character a great deal: polished brings out the crystals, honed and leathered give a more natural feel, and flamed creates a rough surface suited to outdoor use.

Choosing the right granite colour

With 65 references, the easiest approach is to start from the atmosphere you want.

  • Blacks such as Noir Zimbabwe Extra Black or Black Pepper, with its white and grey flecks, are timeless and forgiving of small marks.
  • Whites and pale greys such as Kashmir White or Silver Cloud brighten a kitchen but need well-maintained sealing.
  • Blues such as Azul Aran or Bleu Labrador Extra give spectacular iridescence on an island.
  • Browns and greens such as Baltic Brown or Vert Ubatuba pair well with wood and kitchens full of character.

Not every finish is available for every colour: the catalogue below shows, for each granite, whether it comes polished, honed, leathered or flamed.

Your questions

What thickness should I choose for a granite worktop?

We cut granite in 20 or 30 mm. 20 mm gives a slimmer, more contemporary line and reduces the load on the units. 30 mm has a more substantial presence, popular on islands or in traditional-style kitchens. Our surveyors will advise you based on any overhangs, the position of the sink and the look you want for the edge.

Is a leathered finish suitable for a granite worktop?

Yes. A leathered finish gives granite a slightly undulating feel and a matt look that shows off the stone without reflections. It shows fingerprints less than a polished finish, which is appealing on dark colours. It needs slightly more careful cleaning in the micro-texture and regular sealing. It is not available on every colour.
