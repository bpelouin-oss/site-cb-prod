MStone ceramic worktops combine the toughness of a ceramic fired at very high temperature with a palette of 13 carefully designed patterns. Certified for food contact and stable in sunlight, it suits a kitchen as well as a bathroom. As MStone Surfaces partners, we shape it in Mafra.

Worktops in MStone Ceramic

MStone ceramic is a technical surface made from natural raw materials fired at very high temperature. Non-porous, it needs no sealing and resists heat, scratches and chemicals, with colours that hold up in sunlight. It suits worktops, splashbacks, vanity tops and wall cladding, with the 12 mm format ideal for walls.

Inspiration

Why choose MStone ceramic for your worktop

MStone ceramic ticks every practical box: no porosity, so no treatment; good resistance to heat, scratches and chemicals; colour that stays stable even when exposed to UV. Its certification for food contact reassures anyone who prepares pastry and dough directly on the worktop.

What makes it original is its four finishes. Lux is a bright polish, Nat a natural matt, Soft a matt that is gentle to the touch, and Soft 3D a textured matt that adds depth to stone effects. The same design can therefore change character from room to room.

With two thicknesses, 12 and 20 mm, it follows your project from worktop to vanity top, with 12 mm particularly well suited to wall cladding.

Choosing your MStone ceramic colour

The 13 MStone designs are split between marble effects, soft stones and bolder shades.

  • For bright marble: Calacatta Gold with golden veins, or the more restrained Calacatta Cendre with ash-grey veins.
  • For a natural, warm feel: Travertin Créma, Créma Natural or Sahara, which go well with wood and stone.
  • For character: Cosmos, a speckled dark grey, Ombra di Pietra, or Emeraude Green for a rare touch of colour.
  • For a precious touch: Antique Oro and its aged gold effect.

In a bathroom, the Soft or Nat finish avoids a mirror effect and feels more soothing. In a kitchen, Lux best reveals the depth of the veining.

Your questions

What is the Soft 3D finish on MStone ceramic?

Soft 3D is a textured matt finish: the surface has a light relief that follows the design, much like brushed natural stone. It gives a very authentic look to stone effects and limits reflections. It simply needs cleaning with a soft sponge to reach into the micro-texture. It is not offered on every colour: the catalogue below shows which.

Can I knead and roll pastry directly on MStone ceramic?

Yes. MStone ceramic is certified for food contact, and its non-porous surface has no pores to trap flour, grease or bacteria. Clean the worktop with soapy water before and after use. For cutting, keep a board: the surface is not easily harmed, but such a hard ceramic quickly dulls knife edges.
