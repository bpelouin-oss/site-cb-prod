What is a ceramic worktop?

You may hear it called ceramic, ultra-compact surface or large-format porcelain. The principle is the same: natural minerals are pressed and then fired, or sintered, at very high temperature. Infinity sinters at over 1,200 °C, and Cosentino uses its exclusive TSP process for Dekton®.

The result is an extremely dense material with almost zero water absorption. With no resin to weaken it, it copes with heat, resists UV and needs no sealing at all. Designs reproduce marble, stone, concrete, oxidised metal or plain colours, in polished, satin or matt finishes.

These surfaces are produced in very large slabs, which means fewer joins, or none at all, on an island or splashback.

Dekton®, Neolith, Infinity, MStone, Materia: comparing ceramics

  • Dekton® by Cosentino: the most complete range, with the 3,300 × 1,630 mm Jumbo format, four design families (Natural, Solid, Tech, Industrial), X-Gloss, matt and Velvet finishes, and thicknesses from 8 to 30 mm.
  • Neolith (The Size range): slabs up to 3,200 × 1,500 mm in 6 and 12 mm, perfect for cladding walls and splashbacks without joins, in polished, matt or Silk satin.
  • Infinity: porcelain made in Italy, known for its marble, stone and concrete effects, in 12 and 20 mm.
  • MStone ceramic: non-porous, certified for food contact, UV stable, with four finishes including the textured Soft 3D.
  • Materia: a short, matt-only collection inspired by concrete, limestone and earth.

Ceramic in the kitchen: advantages and limitations

In our selection guide, ceramic earns top marks on all four criteria: scratches, heat, stains and upkeep. In practice, you can put down a hot pan, cut without worrying about marking the surface and forget a drop of wine without consequence. It also handles outdoor life: terraces, summer kitchens, poolsides.

It does have a few limitations worth knowing. Its hardness blunts knives, so a chopping board is still advisable for your blades. The very rigid edges and corners can chip under a violent knock, for example a cast-iron pot dropped on the edge. Finally, cutting it takes precise expertise, which we handle in the factory with our CNC machines.

Choosing a slightly rounded or chamfered edge reduces this risk in everyday use.

Best uses and care for a ceramic worktop

Ceramic suits almost everything: hard-working kitchen worktops, large-format islands, matching splashbacks in 6 or 12 mm, vanity tops, wall cladding, and outdoor kitchens in full sun thanks to its UV stability.

It is the easiest to care for of all the materials we work with:

  • hot water and washing-up liquid for everyday cleaning;
  • an ordinary household cleaner for stubborn marks, as the surface is not affected by common chemicals;
  • no sealing, either at installation or afterwards.

On a matt finish, a metal mark left by a pan comes off with a mildly abrasive cream. Our fitters will show you how when they deliver your worktop.

Your questions

What is the difference between Dekton® and Neolith?

Both are highly resistant ultra-compact ceramics. Dekton® is made by Cosentino using its TSP process, in a 3,300 × 1,630 mm Jumbo format and thicknesses from 8 to 30 mm. Neolith, which we offer through its The Size range, focuses on slabs up to 3,200 × 1,500 mm in 6 and 12 mm, popular for splashbacks and walls. The choice often comes down to the design you prefer.

Can a ceramic worktop be installed outdoors?

Yes, it is one of its great strengths. Ceramic resists UV, its colour stays stable in the sun and its near-zero water absorption makes it largely unaffected by the weather. Dekton® explicitly states that it can be used outdoors, and both MStone ceramic and Infinity are described as UV stable. It is the material we recommend for a summer kitchen or a built-in barbecue.

Can a ceramic worktop break?

Once laid on a flat, well-supported base, a ceramic worktop is very strong in daily use. The weak point is the edges and corners: a violent knock from a heavy object can cause a chip. That is why we take great care over cutting in the factory, on-site measuring and the choice of edge profile, and why installation is carried out by our own teams.
