Inspiration

Why specify Texta granite

Texta granite wins people over by touch first. Its velvety matt finish makes you want to run your hand over the top, and it changes how the stone is perceived: the crystals stay visible, but without reflections, as if softened.

It is also a practical choice. On polished black granite every fingerprint shows; on Texta, the surface is known for resisting fingerprints and everyday marks are discreet. The stone keeps its Mohs hardness of 6 to 7 and its heat resistance, ideal near the hob.

Texta granite is available in 20 or 30 mm. It goes particularly well with matt fronts, raw wood, brushed steel and minimalist or industrial kitchens. We fabricate it to your drawings, with cut-outs, edges and hand finishing.

Texta granite colours: guiding the choice

The Texta finish transforms classic granites. Absolute Black becomes a deep black with a velvet touch, without the reflections of a polish: it is the benchmark for a matt black kitchen. Titanium and Titanium Gold, with their bold veins, take on a softer, more sophisticated look.

For a light kitchen, Rebel White and Blanc Cristal offer bright backgrounds, while Dalmata, flecked with black, adds a graphic touch. Warm tones such as Brown Silk, Antique Brown or Baltic Brown go well with dark woods. Azul Aran®, a blue-grey, makes a very handsome island.

The matt finish changes how colours are perceived compared with a polished sample: to confirm the shade with your client, always rely on a sample or slab in the Texta finish.

Your questions

Is Texta granite harder to clean than polished granite?

No, it is cleaned in the same way, with warm water and mild soap on a non-abrasive sponge. Its matt surface hides fingerprints better than a polish. As with any matt finish, a greasy residue can show in raking light: a pass with a slightly damp microfibre cloth makes it disappear. Strippers and abrasive creams should be avoided.

Texta granite or honed granite: what is the difference?

Both are matt, but not in the same way. Honed is achieved by stopping the polishing before it reaches a shine: it is understated and natural. Texta is a specific Cosentino treatment that gives a velvety, silky and even softer feel, and a surface less prone to fingerprints. The best approach is to compare both samples by touch with your client.
