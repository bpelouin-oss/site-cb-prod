Inspiration

Why choose Texta granite for your worktop

Texta granite wins people over by touch first. Its velvety matt finish makes you want to rest your hand on the worktop, and it changes the way the stone is perceived: the crystals remain visible, but without reflections, as if softened.

It is also a practical choice. On polished black granite every fingerprint shows; Texta is designed to resist fingerprints, and everyday marks stay discreet. The stone keeps its Mohs hardness of 6 to 7 and its heat tolerance, ideal near the hob.

Texta granite is available in 20 or 30 mm. It pairs particularly well with matt fronts, raw wood, brushed steel and minimalist or industrial-style kitchens.

Choosing your Texta granite colour

The Texta finish transforms classic granites. Absolute Black becomes a deep black with a velvet touch, free of polished reflections: the benchmark for a matt black kitchen. Titanium and Titanium Gold, with their bold veining, take on a softer, more sophisticated look.

For a light kitchen, Rebel White and Blanc Cristal offer bright backgrounds, while Dalmata, flecked with black, adds a graphic touch. Warm tones such as Brown Silk, Antique Brown or Baltic Brown pair well with dark woods. Azul Aran®, a blue-grey, makes a very handsome island.

The matt result changes how colours look compared with a polished sample: always ask for a Texta sample to judge the true shade.

Your questions

Is Texta granite harder to clean than polished granite?

No, it is cleaned in the same way, with warm water, mild soap and a non-abrasive sponge. Its matt surface hides fingerprints better than polished granite. As with any matt finish, greasy residue can show in raking light: a slightly damp microfibre cloth makes it disappear. Avoid strippers and abrasive creams.

Texta granite or honed granite: what is the difference?

Both are matt, but not in the same way. Honed is achieved by stopping the polishing before it reaches a shine: it is understated and natural. Texta is a specific Cosentino treatment that gives a velvety, silky feel, softer still, and a surface less prone to fingerprints. The best way to decide is to compare both samples with your fingertips.
