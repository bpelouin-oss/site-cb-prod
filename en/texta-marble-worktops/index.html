Inspiration

Why specify Texta marble

Texta marble resolves a common hesitation for the end client: they love marble but dread the first coffee stain. The surface treatment applied by Cosentino significantly improves its stain resistance, making it more relaxed to live with than untreated marble.

It is important to stay clear, though: the treatment does not change the nature of the stone. Texta marble remains a metamorphic rock formed from limestone, with a Mohs hardness of 3 to 4, sensitive to acids and scratches. Lemon, vinegar or a descaler can still dull it.

It is therefore ideally suited to bathrooms, tables, consoles, splashbacks or display islands, and to kitchens for those willing to take a few precautions. We fabricate it in 20 or 30 mm, with cut-outs and holes to your drawings.

Lunar White, Rose Bescato or Venano

The collection has three marbles, each with a very distinct personality.

  • Lunar White: a moonlike white with soft grey veins, bright and calming, perfect for a light bathroom or a white kitchen.
  • Venano: a white veined with grey in flowing lines, more graphic, which shows off an island or a large vanity top.
  • Rose Bescato: a salmon pink with white and cream fragments, for a warm and singular room, superb with brass.

Each marble comes honed, leathered or polished, in 20 or 30 mm. In kitchens, honed or leathered hide any acid marks better than polished. In bathrooms or on a table, polished reveals the full depth of the veins. A factory visit by appointment lets you see the slabs.

Your questions

Is Texta marble still sensitive to lemon?

Yes. The Texta treatment improves stain resistance, meaning the penetration of coloured liquids, but it does not make marble immune to acids. Lemon juice, vinegar or a descaler can leave a dull mark on the surface. Guidance for the client: wipe up these liquids quickly and clean with a mild, pH-neutral soap.

Can a vanity top be made in Texta marble?

It is one of its best uses. In a bathroom, marble faces fewer food acids and knocks, and the Texta treatment limits rings from cosmetics. We fabricate the top to measure with the basin cut-outs and tap holes shown on your drawings, in 20 or 30 mm. Gentle cleaners are preferable to acidic descalers.
