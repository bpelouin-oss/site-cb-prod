Every project in this gallery was drawn, cut and finished for one specific space. Family kitchens, bathrooms, tables, bars and restaurants: browse our worktop projects and pick out the materials, edges and cut-outs that inspire you for your own home or business.

Worktop projects for every room

Kitchens are where we work most often: islands with rounded corners that extend into a dining table, splashbacks cut from the same slab as the worktop, returns and fronts clad in stone. In bathrooms we craft vanity tops, wall cladding and integrated basins that create one continuous piece.

The gallery also features tables, bar and restaurant counters, a fireplace and an office. These projects call for particular care over edges, thicknesses and durability, because the surface is seen and touched all day long.

You will mostly spot marble and quartzite, two stones whose veining gives each piece its character. Yet we work every material family with the same standards: quartz, ceramic, Corian®, Minéralis and recycled stone.

Using the gallery to plan your project

Filter the projects by type of room, then read the caption under each photo: it names the material and often the colour used. It is the easiest way to put a name to a shade or vein pattern you like.

Save the images that speak to you and attach them to your quote request, with a few words on what draws you: the colour, the shape of an edge, a matching splashback, an integrated basin. Your adviser will grasp what you have in mind much more quickly.

Bear in mind that natural stone is never identical from one slab to the next. The best way to choose is still to come and see the slabs available at our factory in Igreja Nova, by appointment, before you sign off your worktop.

Your questions

Can you make a worktop identical to one in your gallery?

We can reproduce the shape, thicknesses, edges and cut-outs of a project you like. With natural stone such as marble or quartzite, however, the veining will never be exactly the same, because every slab is unique. We will show you the slabs available so you can find a close match, or suggest a more uniform engineered surface if consistency matters to you.

Do you make worktops for bars and restaurants?

Yes. Bar counters, restaurant tables and service tops are among our projects. For a space open to the public, we help you choose a material that copes with heavy use and daily cleaning. Fit-out professionals also benefit from preferential terms and a dedicated contact who follows the job from start to finish.
