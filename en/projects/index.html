What we make for your projects

Kitchens remain our most frequent work: worktops, islands with rounded corners extended into a table, splashbacks cut from the same slab as the worktop, returns and island ends clad in stone. For bathrooms, we make vanity tops, integrated basins and wall cladding that form one continuous whole.

The gallery also shows tables, bar and restaurant counters, a fireplace and a desk. These pieces call for particular care over edges, thicknesses and joints, because the surface is seen and touched all the time.

You will mostly see marble and quartzite, but we work every one of our material families to the same standard: granite, quartz, ceramic, Corian®, Minéralis and recycled stone. Each piece was checked for dimensions and finish before it left the factory.

Using the gallery with your clients

Filter the projects by type of room and read the caption under each photo: it gives the material and often the colour. It is a simple way to show your client a look, an edge or a cut-out before finalising their project.

When you send us an enquiry, attach the reference images to your dimensioned drawings. We grasp the expected result faster and can flag a technical constraint at the study stage, before the quote reaches your client.

Remind them too that natural stone is never identical from one slab to the next. For a marble or quartzite with strong veining, the safest option is to come and choose the slab at our Igreja Nova factory, by appointment, on your own or with your client, before production starts.

Your questions

Can you make a piece identical to one in the gallery?

Yes. Working from your dimensioned drawings, we can reproduce the shape, thicknesses, edges and cut-outs of a project. With natural stone such as marble or quartzite, however, the veining will never be strictly identical, because every slab is unique. We show you the slabs available to get close to the look, or a more uniform engineered surface if consistency matters to your client.

Do you make pieces for bars, hotels and restaurants?

Yes. Bar counters, restaurant tables and service tops are among our projects. For premises open to the public, we help you choose a material suited to heavy use and daily cleaning. We manufacture from your drawings, check every piece and deliver it to the address you give us, while the work on site remains with your own teams or partners.
