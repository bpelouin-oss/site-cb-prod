Inspiration

Why specify Sensa quartzite

Untreated quartzite is already an excellent kitchen stone. Sensa quartzite goes further thanks to Sensa Protect, Cosentino's long-lasting stain treatment: oils and coloured drinks penetrate far less, even on light shades where untreated quartzite would call for vigilance.

The stone keeps its natural qualities. It takes hot pans, resists UV with stable colour and scratches less than granite. Each slab remains unique, with the veining specific to its quarry of origin.

Two finishes are available: Mir, a bright polish that reveals the depth of the veins, and Suède, a soft, natural matt. Thicknesses of 20 and 30 mm allow anything from a slim worktop to a chunky island. We plan the cutting of each slab from your drawings to place the veins to best effect.

The six Sensa quartzite colours

The six Sensa quartzites cover a tight but very expressive palette.

  • Taj Mahal: a warm cream with soft golden veins, probably the easiest to pair, from light oak to brass.
  • Nahoa: a creamy white with grey and gold veins, for a light, refined kitchen.
  • White Macaubas: a white crossed by blue-grey waves, fresher and more graphic.
  • Platino: a platinum grey with silvery ripples, elegant with stainless steel and grey fronts.
  • Vancouver: a greyish brown with light beige veins, warm and understated.
  • Nilo: a deep black with delicate light veins, for an island that makes a statement.

On a quartzite slab the pattern can vary noticeably: approving the slab before cutting is recommended, for example during a factory visit by appointment.

Your questions

Sensa quartzite or untreated quartzite: which to specify?

Both offer the same hardness and the same heat resistance. Untreated quartzite has a much wider choice of colours but needs resealing from time to time. Sensa quartzite narrows the choice to six shades and frees the user from that constraint thanks to Sensa Protect. For a light colour in a family kitchen, Sensa is often the most relaxed solution.

Is Sensa quartzite suitable for a very sunny room?

Yes. Cosentino states UV resistance and stable colour for Sensa quartzite, which makes it a good choice in front of a large window or in a conservatory. That is an advantage over resin-based surfaces, which can change in the sun. For a fully outdoor kitchen, mention it in your request: we assess the project case by case.
