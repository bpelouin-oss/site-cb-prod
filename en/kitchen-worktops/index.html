Kitchen worktops manufactured from your drawings

Kitchen studios, interior contractors and stonemasons trust us to manufacture their bespoke kitchen worktops. Working from your drawings and dimensions, our Mafra factory cuts, machines and finishes each piece in granite, quartz, ceramic or Minéralis, sink and hob cut-outs included, then delivers it across France, Portugal and internationally.

Which materials for a kitchen worktop?

In a kitchen, the worktop works harder than anywhere else. These are the families we manufacture most often for our kitchen studio clients:

  • ceramic (Dekton®, Infinity, MStone), for the busiest kitchens: it tolerates heat, scratches and stains;
  • granite, for heat and impact resistance, sealed regularly, or as Sensa treated granite to do without;
  • quartz, non-porous and valued for its minimal upkeep, with a trivet for very hot pans;
  • Minéralis, which gives the look of quartz with 85% recycled glass and heat resistance up to 180 °C.

Your client dreams of marble? It remains sensitive to lemon, vinegar and wine. A quartzite or a marble-effect ceramic offers similar veining with far fewer precautions day to day.

Sink and hob cut-outs from your drawings

Every opening is CNC machined to the dimensions and positions shown on your drawings. Depending on the material, several options are available:

  • an undermount sink cut-out, with polished edges visible around the bowl;
  • a sink carved from the solid material;
  • drainer grooves engraved beside the bowl;
  • a hob cut-out matched to the specified model;
  • holes for taps, a socket or a soap dispenser.

The edges of each cut-out are then finished by hand for a clean result. To avoid back-and-forth, include the exact sink, hob and tap references in your file, or their technical data sheets: we work from the dimensions supplied by their manufacturers.

Thicknesses, edges and finishes

Depending on the range, we work from slim, contemporary 12 mm up to more generous 20 and 30 mm; some ceramics even come in 8 mm. Depending on the material, a thin worktop can be given a thickened edge for a solid look without adding weight to the piece.

Next comes the edge profile: straight and crisp, slightly rounded for a softer feel, or chamfered. All edges are finished by hand in our workshop after CNC cutting.

Then there is the surface finish. A high polish gives colours depth. Honed or matt finishes look more understated. Leather and textured finishes have a tactile feel and show fewer fingerprints and water marks. Specify these choices on your drawings: profile, thickness and finish for each piece.

What to send us

CB Prod manufactures solely from the drawings, dimensions and technical information supplied by its trade clients. Site surveys therefore remain with you or your partners. For an accurate quote and a smooth production run, your file should ideally include:

  • a dimensioned drawing of each piece, for example in DXF, DWG or PDF;
  • the material, range, colour, thickness and finish;
  • the profile of visible edges;
  • the position and references of the sink, hob and taps;
  • any matching splashbacks, upstands or end panels;
  • the delivery address and access conditions.

Our technical team studies your files, flags any inconsistency or missing information, then optimises how each piece is cut from the slab before production starts. You receive your quote within 48 working hours.

Quality control and delivery

Before leaving the factory, every worktop is checked: dimensions, cut-out positions, edge consistency and finish quality are compared with your drawings. This check limits unpleasant surprises when your own teams or partners carry out the work on site.

The pieces are then securely packed to protect edges and corners in transit, which matters for long worktops or pieces with large cut-outs. We deliver across France, Portugal and internationally.

As manufacturing partner to the CB Surfaces group companies, CB Prod also works for independent kitchen studios and interior contractors. You are welcome to visit our Igreja Nova factory by appointment, Monday to Friday, to see the slabs and meet the team who will make your projects.

Your questions

In what format should I send my kitchen drawings?

We work with the usual formats, for example DXF, DWG or dimensioned PDF. What matters is that every piece is dimensioned, with cut-out positions, edge profiles and vein direction where relevant. A complete file lets us reply within 48 working hours and optimise how pieces are cut from the slab.

Do you come on site to take dimensions?

No. CB Prod works solely on bespoke manufacturing and delivery. Orders are produced from the drawings and dimensions you send us. The final survey is best carried out once the base units are in place and level, by your own teams or partners, and we then manufacture to that survey.

Which thickness should I recommend for a kitchen worktop?

20 mm, available in most of our ranges, suits the majority of kitchens. 30 mm gives a more solid look, often popular on islands. 12 mm, available in ceramic and some quartz ranges, creates a very slim line. Check compatibility with the sink, hob and units: we can review this with you.

Can a private homeowner order a worktop from you?

CB Prod manufactures for professionals: kitchen studios, stonemasons, architects, interior contractors and building firms. If you are a homeowner, the simplest route is through your kitchen studio or stonemason, who will send us their drawings. You can also turn to the CB Surfaces group brands: CB Surfaces, Easy Plan and Kitchenfab.
