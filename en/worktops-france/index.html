Delivery and fitting

Your bespoke worktop, delivered and fitted at your home in France

Looking for a bespoke worktop delivered and fitted in France? CB Prod makes it in its own factory in Mafra, Portugal, then delivers and installs it anywhere in France with its own teams. A French phone line makes us easy to reach.

Request a free quote →Call +33 (1) 88 32 64 49

A bespoke worktop made in Portugal

Every worktop we supply to France is made in our factory at Igreja Nova, near Mafra. We have been working natural stone, quartz, ceramic, Corian®, Minéralis and recycled stone there, drawing on family craftsmanship handed down over more than three generations.

Your piece is CNC-cut to a tenth of a millimetre, then its edges, corners and cut-outs are finished by hand by our craftsmen. Undermount sink, flush hob, matching splashback, integrated basin: every detail is prepared in the workshop from the measurements taken in your home.

Being the manufacturer means we stay in control of the whole process. The person who prepares your quote works alongside those who cut and fit your worktop, and lead times are confirmed in the proposal.

Delivery and fitting anywhere in France by our own teams

We deliver and fit anywhere in France with our own teams. Our fitters are trained on our materials and used to handling heavy, delicate pieces.

The process is the same as in Portugal. Once you accept the quote, our surveyors come to take the exact measurements on site. Production starts from that survey, then our fitters arrive to adjust, fix and seal your worktop with care.

Before leaving, they give you care advice suited to your material. You also benefit from the warranties of the manufacturers we work with: up to 25 years depending on the range, and 20 years on Minéralis.

A French phone line and supply-only option

For any questions, a French line is available on +33 (1) 88 32 64 49, Monday to Friday from 8:00 am to 5:30 pm. You can also email direction@cbproducao.com with your plans and photos: an expert reviews your request and sends you a free quote within 48 working hours.

Already working with a kitchen designer or a fitter you trust? Supply only is available. We then make your worktop from the measurements provided and prepare it for delivery, while your professional takes care of fitting.

Kitchen studios, architects and builders based in France can also join our partner network and benefit from preferential terms for their projects.

Your questions

Do you work everywhere in France?

Yes, we deliver and fit anywhere in France with our own teams. Simply give the site address in your quote request and we will organise the survey, delivery and fitting accordingly. If you already have a fitter, we can also supply the worktop on its own.

How long does it take to get a worktop in France?

Lead time depends on the material, the availability of the chosen slab, the complexity of the cut-outs and the fitting schedule. That is why we state it in the quote and commit to it. Production only begins after the on-site survey, which guarantees a worktop that fits your kitchen perfectly.

Can my kitchen designer order directly from you?

Yes. We work with many professionals, including kitchen designers, architects and builders. Your kitchen designer can send us their drawings, choose the material with you and decide whether our teams fit the worktop or they handle it themselves. Trade customers enjoy preferential terms and a dedicated contact to follow the order.
