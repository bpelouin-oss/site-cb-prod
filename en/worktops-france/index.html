Manufacturing and delivery

Made in Portugal, delivered throughout France

Are you a trade professional based in France looking for a bespoke worktop manufacturer? CB Prod makes your pieces in its Mafra factory in Portugal, from your drawings, then delivers them anywhere in France. Setting the pieces in place remains with you or your partners, and a French phone line keeps communication simple.

Worktops made in Portugal for your projects in France

Every piece we deliver to France is made in our Igreja Nova factory near Mafra. There we work natural stone, quartz, ceramic, Corian®, Minéralis and recycled stone, with family craftsmanship handed down over more than three generations.

Your pieces are CNC cut from your dimensioned drawings, then their edges, corners and cut-outs are finished by hand by our craftsmen. Undermount sink, flush hob, matching splashback, integrated basin: every detail is prepared in the workshop according to the technical information you send us.

Being the manufacturer lets us keep control of the whole chain, from slab sourcing to final inspection. The person who studies your quote works alongside those who fabricate your pieces, and the manufacturing lead time is given to you with the proposal.

Delivery anywhere in France, setting in place on your side

We deliver anywhere in France, to the address you give us: your workshop, your depot or straight to your client's site. Every piece is inspected before dispatch for dimensions, cut-outs, edges and surface appearance, then securely packed to withstand transport.

Let us know in your enquiry about any access constraints and your preferred timing, so we can organise delivery accordingly. On receipt, we advise you to check the condition of the packages and pieces in the presence of the carrier.

CB Prod does not take measurements on site or set pieces in place. Surveying and putting the pieces in place remain your responsibility or that of your partners. You keep the full relationship with your end client, and we stay focused on what we do best: manufacturing accurately and delivering in good condition.

A French phone line and close follow-up of your files

For any questions, a French phone line is available on +33 (1) 88 32 64 49, Monday to Friday from 8.00am to 5.30pm. You can also email direction@cbproducao.com with your dimensioned drawings and technical files attached, for example in DXF, DWG or PDF.

Our team studies your file, checks that the dimensions are consistent, prepares the cutting plan and sends you a quote within 48 working hours. One contact then follows your order, from drawing approval to dispatch.

Kitchen studios, stonemasons, architects, interior contractors and building firms based in France can entrust us with their manufacturing just as they would a local workshop. You are also welcome to visit the Mafra factory by appointment, on your own or with your client, to choose a natural stone slab.

Your questions

Do you deliver throughout France?

Yes, we deliver anywhere in France, to the address of your choice: workshop, depot or site. Include it in your quote request along with any access constraints, so we can organise delivery accordingly. The pieces are inspected and securely packed before they leave our Mafra factory.

Do you handle setting the pieces in place in France?

No. CB Prod works exclusively on bespoke manufacturing and delivery. Taking measurements on site and setting the pieces in place remain your responsibility or that of your partners. We manufacture to your approved drawings, inspect every piece and deliver it ready for your teams to put in place.

What is the lead time for pieces delivered to France?

The lead time depends on the material, slab availability, the complexity of the pieces and the destination. That is why we give it to you with the quote, sent within 48 working hours. Manufacturing starts once the order and final drawings are approved, which avoids last-minute changes to pieces that have already been cut.
