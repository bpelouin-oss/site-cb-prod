What exactly is a quartz worktop?

A quartz worktop is not a stone quarried in a block: it is an engineered surface made in a factory, combining a very high proportion of crushed natural quartz with resins and pigments. With MStone, for example, quartz makes up more than 93% of the material.

This make-up explains its qualities. The surface is non-porous, so liquids do not soak in, and the pattern stays consistent from slab to slab, which makes long runs and joints easier. Manufacturers reproduce plain colours as well as very realistic marble effects.

It should not be confused with quartzite, a genuine natural stone quarried from the ground with different properties: a distinction worth explaining to your clients.

Silestone®, Compac, MStone, Stone Italiana: the quartz brands we fabricate

The four brands share the qualities of quartz, but each has its own character.

  • Silestone® by Cosentino: the best known, with HybriQ® technology, polished, matt Suede and textured Volcano finishes, and a very wide choice of colours.
  • Compac: Spanish technological quartz with a Mohs hardness of 7, NSF certified, organised into the Ice Max™, Luxury, Unique and Elegance collections, in 12, 20 and 30 mm.
  • MStone: over 93% natural quartz, polished, matt, leathered and grain finishes, in 12 and 20 mm, also suited to splashbacks and furniture.
  • Stone Italiana: a short selection of Italian quartz inspired by the most sought-after marbles and stones.

We can source the brand specified by your client or the architect; failing that, the choice usually comes down to colour and finish.

Strengths and limits of quartz to explain to the client

Quartz shines in everyday use. Being non-porous, it needs no sealing and resists stains very well: coffee, wine or oil wipe away without a trace. It handles scratches and knocks well, and its consistent colour reassures clients who want a predictable result, including across several homes.

Its main limit comes from its resins: it handles intense heat less well than natural stone or ceramic. A pan straight off the hob should always go on a trivet. For an outdoor kitchen in full sun, ceramic is the better option, as it is designed for that use.

Finally, quartz does not have the one-off character of a stone slab: it is a choice of consistency rather than surprise.

Uses, fabrication and care guidance for quartz

Quartz suits family kitchens: worktops, islands, matching splashbacks and breakfast bars. It also works well in bathrooms, as vanity tops or wall cladding, particularly in 12 mm from Compac and MStone.

At the factory we produce, from your drawings, the sink and hob cut-outs, tap holes, edges and matching pieces, then check every piece before packing and delivery.

Guidance to pass on to the end user:

  • a microfibre cloth or soft sponge with water and neutral soap for daily cleaning;
  • a gentle cleaner suitable for quartz for limescale marks;
  • no harsh strippers, strong solvents or abrasive pads;
  • a chopping board for cutting and a trivet for hot pans.

Your questions

Is quartz suitable for a kitchen that sees heavy cooking?

Yes for stains and upkeep, with a caveat on heat. Quartz copes with the ordinary heat of a kitchen, but its resins make it less tolerant than granite or ceramic when faced with a very hot pan. If your client cooks a great deal and will not use trivets, ceramic or treated granite are alternatives to suggest at the quotation stage.

Can you fabricate a quartz island with no visible joint?

It depends on the island's dimensions and the slab size, for example 3,200 × 1,600 mm with Compac. When the island is within the size of one slab, there is no joint on the top. Beyond that, we work out the joint position from your drawings and submit it to you for approval, and the consistency of quartz allows a very discreet join.

Can you supply several quartz brands for the same project?

Yes. We fabricate Silestone®, Compac, MStone and Stone Italiana, and we handle sourcing the material from the suppliers. You can therefore combine, for example, a Silestone® kitchen worktop and 12 mm MStone vanity tops in a single order. Simply state the brand, colour and finish of each item on your drawings.
