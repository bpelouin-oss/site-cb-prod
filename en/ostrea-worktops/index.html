Inspiration

Why choose Ostrea for your worktop

Ostrea appeals to people who want a material with a story to tell. Every year the seafood industry produces large quantities of shells; Ostrea crushes them and binds them with an eco-compatible resin to create a surface for kitchens and bathrooms.

The result is dense and non-porous: no sealing is needed, and cleaning is simply water and a mild soap. The shell fragments catch the light differently at different times of day, with pearly glints that give real depth to what is in fact a matt surface.

Ostrea comes in a honed finish and 20 mm. Like any resin-bound surface, it appreciates a trivet for hot pans and a board for cutting.

Huîtres or Saint Jacques: choosing your Ostrea colour

The two Ostrea colours are named after their original shell, and they look quite distinct.

Huîtres is a slightly greyish white with a fine grain, dotted with dark flecks and pearly glints. Understated and bright, it goes with white, grey or navy fronts and works very well in a seaside-style kitchen or a contemporary bathroom.

Saint Jacques is an off-white scattered with beige, brown, golden and pearly pink chips. Warmer, it pairs with pale wood, rattan, brass and earthy tones.

The natural fleck hides small everyday marks well. We recommend seeing a sample in person, as the pearly glints are hard to capture in photos.

Your questions

Is Ostrea suitable for a kitchen worktop?

Yes, its dense, non-porous surface does not let liquids soak in and is easy to clean. It suits a kitchen used for everyday cooking, with the right habits: a trivet for hot dishes, a chopping board, and no abrasive products on the honed finish. For very heavy use or an outdoor kitchen, we will compare other materials with you.

Can an Ostrea worktop have a high-gloss polish?

In our catalogue, Ostrea is offered only in a honed finish, in 20 mm. It is this soft matt finish that best shows off the shell fragments and their pearly glints, without a mirror effect. If you are looking for a recycled surface with a high polish, take a look at MStone or Compac recycled stone, both of which offer a polished finish.
