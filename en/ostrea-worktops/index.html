Ostrea is probably the most poetic surface in our catalogue. Inspiration

Why specify Ostrea

Ostrea appeals to projects that want a material with a story: a shop, a restaurant, a hotel bathroom or a kitchen with character. Every year the seafood industry produces large quantities of shells; Ostrea crushes them and binds them with an eco-compatible resin to make a kitchen or bathroom surface.

The result is dense and non-porous: no sealing to plan for, and simple cleaning with water and mild soap. The shell fragments catch the light differently depending on the time of day, with a pearly sheen that gives great depth to what is a matt surface.

Ostrea is available in a honed finish and in 20 mm. Like any resin-bound surface, it needs a trivet for hot pans and a board for cutting.

Huîtres or Saint Jacques: the two Ostrea colours

The two Ostrea colours are named after the shell they come from, and each has a distinct look.

Huîtres is a slightly greyish white with a fine grain, dotted with dark flecks and pearly glints. Understated and bright, it goes with white, grey or navy fronts and suits a seaside-style kitchen as well as a contemporary bathroom.

Saint Jacques is an off-white studded with pearly beige, brown, gold and pink chips. Warmer, it pairs with light wood, rattan, brass and earthy tones.

The natural fleck hides small everyday marks well. The pearly sheen does not come across well in photos: to confirm the choice with your client, rely on a real sample.

Your questions

Is Ostrea suitable for a kitchen worktop?

Yes, its dense, non-porous surface does not let liquids soak in and is easy to clean. It suits a kitchen with moderate use, with the right habits: trivets for hot dishes, a chopping board and no abrasive products on the honed finish. For a very intensive or outdoor kitchen, ceramic is the better option: we fabricate both.

Is Ostrea available in a high-gloss polish?

In our catalogue, Ostrea is available only in a honed finish, in 20 mm. It is this soft matt finish that best shows off the shell fragments and their pearly sheen, without a mirror effect. If your project calls for a recycled surface in high gloss, MStone or Compac recycled stone are two alternatives available in a polished finish.
