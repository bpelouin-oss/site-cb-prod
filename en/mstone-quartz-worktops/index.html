MStone quartz worktops stand out for a choice of finishes that is rare in quartz: polished, matt, leathered or grain. With 23 colours and two thicknesses, it adapts to a kitchen as easily as to a splashback, wall or piece of furniture. As MStone Surfaces partners, we shape it to measure in Mafra.

Worktops in MStone Quartz

MStone quartz is a composite surface made of over 93% natural quartz bound with high-performance resins. Non-porous and resistant to scratches, impacts and stains, it keeps a consistent look from slab to slab. It suits worktops, splashbacks, wall cladding and furniture.

Inspiration

Why choose MStone quartz for your worktop

MStone quartz offers everything you expect from good quartz: a non-porous, hygienic surface, good resistance to scratches and knocks, and no sealing needed. Its composition of over 93% natural quartz gives it a convincingly mineral texture.

What really sets it apart is the finishes. Polished plays on shine, matt calms reflections, leathered adds a light texture to the touch and grain an even more mineral feel. On the same colour, these finishes completely change the mood of the kitchen.

The two thicknesses, 12 and 20 mm, let you carry the worktop up the splashback, clad a wall or make a furniture top in the same material, with a consistent look from slab to slab.

Choosing your MStone quartz colour

The MStone palette meets three main desires.

  • Pure whites such as Bianco Artico or Blanc Extra, for a clean kitchen where the worktop stays in the background.
  • Marble effects such as Calacatta Oro Statuario, Attera Gold with its bold golden veins, or Calacatta Viola, for an island that draws the eye.
  • More original designs such as Terrazzo Perle, the concrete-inspired Concrete Cloud sablé, or Adora, a deep green background with white and gold veins.

A tip from the workshop: on a veined colour, a leathered or matt finish softens reflections and looks closer to stone, while polished heightens the contrast of the veins. Ask us for samples in two finishes to compare.

Your questions

What is the difference between MStone quartz and Minéralis?

Both come from MStone Surfaces and share several colour names, such as Calacatta Taj or Travertino, but their make-up differs. MStone quartz contains over 93% natural quartz. Minéralis replaces much of that quartz with 85% first-production recycled glass, with a low silica content and a 20-year warranty. The visual result is very similar.

Are leathered or grain finishes harder to look after?

Slightly more than polished, but nothing demanding. The light texture can hold limescale or grease in its hollows: a soft sponge with neutral soap followed by a microfibre cloth is enough in most cases. In return, these finishes show fingerprints less and feel very pleasant to the touch.
