Inspiration

Why Materia for your projects

Materia starts from a simple idea: to offer the look of polished concrete, limestone or terracotta without their drawbacks. Real concrete cracks and stains, and terracotta soaks up liquids; ceramic stays stable and needs no treatment.

The collection is deliberately short and matt only. No polish, no shine: a surface that is easy on the eye, does not reflect spotlights and shows off wood, brushed metal or matt lacquered fronts.

The two thicknesses, 12 and 20 mm, let you adapt the worktop to the style: 12 mm for a very slim, architectural line, 20 mm for a stronger presence and more freedom for overhangs. Cut-outs, edges and matching pieces are machined to your drawings.

The four Materia colours

Each of the four shades sets a different mood.

  • Ciment: a warm concrete grey with beige nuances, the most versatile. It works with light oak as well as with black.
  • Calcare: a creamy white inspired by limestone, ideal for a bright kitchen with a Mediterranean spirit.
  • Terra: a pinkish terracotta with natural pigments, for a warm, artisan touch, lovely with brass and sage green.
  • Carbone: a deep anthracite, for a graphic kitchen or an island that needs to stand out.

These matt tones go particularly well with natural materials. To confirm the look with your client, the most telling approach is to place the sample next to the chosen fronts and flooring.

Your questions

Does a matt ceramic stain more than a polished one?

No, ceramic remains non-porous whatever its finish: liquids do not soak in. The difference is visual. On matt, grease or limescale marks can show more in raking light, and metal marks left by pans are more common. They come off with a suitable household cleaner without damaging the surface: useful guidance to pass on.

Is Materia suitable for a bathroom?

Absolutely. Its non-porous surface copes with humidity, cosmetics and everyday cleaning products. 12 mm works well for a slim vanity top or wall cladding, and the Calcare or Ciment shades create a mineral spa feel. We produce the basin and tap cut-outs to your drawings at our Mafra factory.
