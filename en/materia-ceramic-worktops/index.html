Inspiration

Why choose Materia for your worktop

Materia starts from a simple idea: to offer the look of polished concrete, limestone or terracotta without their drawbacks. Real concrete cracks and stains, terracotta soaks up liquids; ceramic stays stable and needs no treatment.

The collection is deliberately short and exclusively matt. No polish, no shine: a surface that is easy on the eye, does not reflect spotlights and sets off wood, brushed metal or matt lacquered fronts.

The two thicknesses, 12 and 20 mm, let you adapt the worktop to the style: 12 mm for a very slim, architectural line, 20 mm for a stronger presence and more freedom for overhangs.

Choosing your Materia colour

Each of the four shades tells a different story.

  • Ciment: a warm concrete grey with beige hints, the most versatile. It works with pale oak as well as with black.
  • Calcare: a creamy white inspired by limestone, ideal for a bright, Mediterranean-style kitchen.
  • Terra: a pinkish terracotta with natural pigments, for a warm, crafted touch, lovely with brass and sage green.
  • Carbone: a deep anthracite, for a graphic kitchen or an island that needs to make a statement.

These matt tones pair particularly well with natural materials. To picture the result, we suggest placing the sample next to your cabinet fronts and flooring.

Your questions

Does a matt ceramic stain more than a polished one?

No, ceramic remains non-porous whatever its finish: liquids do not soak in. The difference is visual. On a matt surface, grease or limescale marks can be more visible in raking light, and metal marks left by a pan are more common. They come off with a suitable household cleaner without damaging the surface.

Is Materia suitable for a bathroom?

Absolutely. Its non-porous surface copes with humidity, cosmetics and everyday cleaning products. 12 mm works well for a slim vanity top or wall cladding, and the Calcare or Ciment shades create a mineral spa atmosphere. We make the basin and tap cut-outs to measure in our factory.
