The professionals we manufacture for

We work exclusively for trade customers, who keep the relationship with their own clients:

  • kitchen studios, who entrust us with the worktops, islands and splashbacks of their kitchens;
  • stonemasons, who subcontract part of their fabrication when workloads peak or for materials they do not work themselves;
  • architects and interior designers, looking for a manufacturer able to follow a demanding design;
  • interior contractors and joiners, for the tops, counters and cladding in their schemes;
  • distributors and building firms, for bespoke pieces delivered to their sites.

Each has their own constraints: a schedule to keep, an end client to reassure, a result that matches what was presented. Our role is to manufacture accurately, inspect and deliver on the agreed terms.

Manufacturing partner to the CB Surfaces group and independents

CB Prod is the manufacturing partner of the companies in the CB Surfaces group, which brings together the CB Surfaces, Easy Plan and Kitchenfab brands. Manufacturing for these brands every day calls for rigorous organisation, proven inspection methods and genuinely consistent quality.

We bring the same standards to independent trade customers in France, Portugal and internationally. Whether you order a single piece or regular batches, your drawings are studied with the same care and your pieces go through the same fabrication and inspection steps.

Our ambition is clear: to become a leading manufacturing partner in Europe, known for its craftsmanship, precision, responsiveness and the quality of everything that leaves the factory.

How we work together

Every order follows the same simple, documented process:

  • you send us your dimensioned drawings and technical files, for example in DXF, DWG or PDF, with the material, finishes, edges and cut-outs;
  • we study the file, optimise the cutting plan and flag anything that needs clarifying;
  • you receive a quote within 48 working hours;
  • once the final drawings are approved, production starts in our Mafra factory;
  • every piece is inspected, packed and delivered to the address you give us.

The split of roles is clear: you remain responsible for surveying on site and setting the pieces in place, directly or through your partners. We remain responsible for manufacturing to your approved drawings and for delivery. One contact follows your file from quote to dispatch.

Materials, sourcing and documentation

We select and purchase our materials from international suppliers, so you do not have to manage slab sourcing yourself. Our range covers several families:

  • natural stone: granite, marble and quartzite, including the treated Sensa and Texta versions;
  • quartz: Silestone®, Compac, Stone Italiana and MStone;
  • ceramic: Dekton®, The Size, Materia, Infinity and MStone;
  • Corian®, Minéralis and recycled stone.

For each range, we provide the technical documentation you need for your files and your clients: specifications, available thicknesses and care recommendations. Manufacturers' warranties apply depending on the range, for example 20 years on Minéralis. You can then present your client with a material suited to their budget and use, backed by reliable information.

Your questions

How do I start working with CB Prod?

Email direction@cbproducao.com or call +33 (1) 88 32 64 49 from France, or +351 219 672 216 from Portugal. Tell us about your company, your trade and the kind of projects you handle, then send us a first file with drawings and materials. We study it and reply with a quote within 48 working hours.

Who is responsible for dimensions and setting in place?

You, or your partners. CB Prod does not survey on site or set pieces in place. We manufacture from the drawings and dimensions you approve, after checking that they are consistent and flagging any points we think need confirming. Once delivered and inspected, the pieces are put in place by your own teams or by the firms you work with.

Can I visit the factory with my client?

Of course. We welcome trade customers and their clients to Igreja Nova, Monday to Friday from 8.00am to 5.30pm, by appointment. It is often the best way to approve a natural stone slab, a colour or a finish together, and to clear up any last doubts before production begins.
