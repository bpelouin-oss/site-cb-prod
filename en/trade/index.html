For the trade: a manufacturing partner for your projects

Kitchen studios, architects, builders and distributors: looking for a trade worktop supplier that keeps a close eye on your projects? CB Prod manufactures in its own Mafra factory and offers you preferential terms, an extensive range and a single point of contact.

A trade worktop supplier you can rely on

Every day we work with kitchen studios, interior architects, joiners, renovation contractors and material distributors. Each has their own constraints: a fitting schedule to keep, an end client to reassure, a finish to deliver exactly as presented.

Our pricing is negotiated directly with our sales management and reflects your purchasing volume. As your business with us grows, your terms improve.

You stay in charge of the relationship with your client. We can act purely as a supplier, as a manufacturer who measures and fits on your behalf, or somewhere in between depending on the job. This flexibility lets you shape our role around each project, whether it is a private kitchen or a larger development.

An extensive range with technical documentation

Our trade partners have access to our suppliers' full ranges, grouped into several families:

  • natural stone: granite, marble and quartzite, including the treated Sensa and Texta versions;
  • quartz: Silestone®, Compac, Stone Italiana and MStone;
  • ceramic: Dekton®, The Size, Materia, Infinity and MStone;
  • Corian®, Minéralis and recycled stone.

We work with MStone Surfaces, which distributes Minéralis and MStone large-format ceramic. For these ranges and all the others, we provide the technical documentation you need for your files and your clients.

You can then present your client with a material that suits their budget and use, backed by reliable information on performance and care.

One dedicated contact, from quote to delivery

On site, time lost looking for the right person is expensive. Every trade partner therefore has a single contact who knows your habits, your clients and your finishing standards. They follow your quote requests, orders and production all the way to delivery.

We are preparing an online area for resellers, with your personalised prices, your orders and production tracking. Until it opens, your contact remains available by phone and email.

To join our network, write to direction@cbproducao.com or call us on +33 (1) 88 32 64 49 from France, or +351 219 672 216 from Portugal. Tell us about your business and we will come back to you to agree your terms together.

Your questions

How do I open a trade account with CB Prod?

Contact our management by email at direction@cbproducao.com or by phone, telling us about your company, your trade and the kind of projects you handle. We then agree terms suited to your purchasing volume and introduce you to the contact who will follow your jobs day to day.

Can you supply worktops without fitting?

Yes, supply only is available if you have your own fitters. We then manufacture the piece from your measurements or drawings and prepare it for delivery. If you prefer, our teams can also take the measurements and fit in France or Portugal, taking that part of the job off your hands.

Can I visit the factory with my client?

Of course. We are happy to welcome professionals with their clients at Igreja Nova, Monday to Friday, by appointment. It is often the best way to approve a natural stone slab, a colour or a finish together and settle any last doubts before production starts.
