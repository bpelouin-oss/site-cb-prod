Contacting CB Prod: preparing a first conversation

A first phone call or email is all it takes to get started. Tell us about your company and your trade, then describe the project: type of pieces, intended material, quantities, delivery address and timescale. If you already have dimensioned drawings or technical files, attach them straight to your message.

We come back to you with any questions, then with a quote within 48 working hours. If you are torn between several materials, we explain frankly their strengths and limits for the intended use.

For a visit, we agree on a weekday appointment together and prepare the relevant slabs and samples in advance. Our address: Estrada Nacional N°9, Km 29, Igreja Nova, 2640-339 Mafra, Portugal. Reach us on +33 (1) 88 32 64 49, +351 219 672 216 or at direction@cbproducao.com.

Your questions

Can I reach CB Prod from France without an international call?

Yes. A French line is available on +33 (1) 88 32 64 49, Monday to Friday from 8.00am to 5.30pm. You can also email direction@cbproducao.com with your drawings and technical files attached. Our trade customers in France are looked after in exactly the same way as those in Portugal, from the first enquiry through to delivery.

I am a private individual: can I order directly?

CB Prod manufactures for trade customers, who handle surveying and setting in place for their own clients. If you have a kitchen or bathroom project, please speak to your kitchen studio or stonemason, who can entrust the manufacturing to us. You can also turn to the brands of our group: CB Surfaces, Easy Plan or Kitchenfab.
