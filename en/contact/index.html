Contacting us: what a first conversation looks like

A first call or email is all it takes to get your project started. Tell us about the room, how you use it and the materials you are drawn to: we will then ask a few simple questions to understand your priorities, such as durability, care, appearance or budget.

If you are torn between several materials, we will explain their strengths and limits honestly. We can then suggest a visit to the factory to see the slabs in person, or move straight on to a quote request if you have already made up your mind.

For a visit, we agree a weekday appointment together and prepare the relevant samples in advance. Our address: Estrada Nacional N°9, Km 29, Igreja Nova, 2640-339 Mafra, Portugal. Reach us on +33 (1) 88 32 64 49, +351 219 672 216 or at direction@cbproducao.com.

Your questions

Can I reach CB Prod from France without an international call?

Yes. A French line is available on +33 (1) 88 32 64 49, Monday to Friday from 8:00 am to 5:30 pm. You can also email direction@cbproducao.com with plans and photos of your project attached. Our clients in France are looked after in exactly the same way as those in Portugal, from the first contact to the fitting.

Do I need an appointment to visit the factory?

Yes, visits are by appointment only, Monday to Friday between 8:00 am and 5:30 pm. Letting us know in advance means we can bring out the slabs and samples that match your project and give you the time you need. Call or email us with your availability and the room you are planning.
