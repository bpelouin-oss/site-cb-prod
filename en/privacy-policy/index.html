Your data

Privacy policy

We use your data only to answer your requests and follow up on your project. We never sell it and do not use it for advertising.

Data controller

CBPROD, LDA (CB Prod), Estrada Nacional N°9, Km 29, Igreja Nova, 2640-339 Mafra, Portugal. NIPC PT514935111. Contact for any question about your data: direction@cbproducao.com.

Data we collect

Only what you enter in the "Request a quote" and "Contact" forms:

  • your contact details: first name, last name, email, phone, postcode, company (optional);
  • your project: project type, material, dimensions, finish, delivery, budget, description;
  • the drawings, dimensions and technical files you then send us by email.

We also keep the date of the request. Your IP address is used briefly, in hashed form, to block abusive submissions (spam); it is not stored in clear.

Why we use it

  • to answer your request and prepare your quote (pre-contractual steps taken at your request, GDPR Article 6(1)(b));
  • to follow up on your project and, if you become a customer, to perform the contract (Article 6(1)(b));
  • to meet our accounting and tax obligations for customers (Article 6(1)(c));
  • to protect the website from spam (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f)).

You receive an acknowledgement email after each request. We do not send you newsletters without your consent.

Who has access

Only CB Prod's sales and technical team and, where needed for your project, the CB Surfaces group teams. Our technical providers access it only to run the service: the website host (PlanetHoster), our business email and, in future, our sales management software. No data is sold or passed on to third parties.

How long we keep it

Your requests are kept for 3 years after our last business contact, then deleted. If you become a customer, invoicing records are kept for the period required by Portuguese law (10 years).

Security

The website runs only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Requests are stored outside the public part of the server, and access is restricted to authorised staff.

Your rights

You can at any time ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict its use, object to its processing or receive it in a common format (portability). Write to us at direction@cbproducao.com: we reply within one month.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can complain to the CNPD, the Portuguese data protection authority (www.cnpd.pt), or to the data protection authority of your country of residence.

Cookies and external services

This website uses no advertising cookies or audience measurement tools, so it shows no cookie banner. Fonts are hosted on our own server.

The factory map (OpenStreetMap) only loads if you click "Show map": your IP address is then sent to the OpenStreetMap Foundation. All photos are hosted on our own server.
