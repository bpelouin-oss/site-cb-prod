Inspiration

Why choose Neolith for your worktop

The Size is the Spanish manufacturer of Neolith ceramic. Its slabs reach 3,200 × 1,500 mm, and their slim 6 or 12 mm thickness makes them ideal for dressing a kitchen continuously: worktop, full-height splashback, island fronts and even entire walls.

Like other ceramics, Neolith resists UV, scratches and stains, and needs no treatment. Its reduced thickness gives it a very contemporary look, with slim edges that make cabinets appear lighter.

That slimness has a counterpart: 12 mm used as a worktop must rest on a perfectly flat, continuous support. That is where our on-site measuring and CNC cutting to a tenth of a millimetre make all the difference.

Choosing your Neolith colour

With more than 60 designs, Neolith covers every style. White marble lovers will look at Calacatta Luxe, Estatuario or Calacatta Gold, the last two designed for large seamless formats. For a warmer feel, White Sands, a sandy white with beige hints, or Pietra di Luna offer mineral softness.

More graphic kitchens will find what they need in Basalt Black with its basalt glints, Nero Zimbabwe, or oxidised metal effects such as Iron Copper and Iron Corten. Amazónico, white with green veins, brings a rare botanical touch.

Polished, matt and Silk satin finishes are not all available on every colour, and some references only come in 12 mm: check these details in the catalogue below before you decide.

Your questions

Are The Size and Neolith the same thing?

Yes. The Size is the name of the Spanish manufacturer, and Neolith is the name of its large-format ceramic. On our website the range appears as The Size, but if you are looking for a Neolith worktop, this is it. The colours shown below, such as Calacatta Luxe or Iron Grey, are Neolith references that we cut to measure.

Can I match my worktop and splashback in Neolith?

It is actually one of the range's strengths. The lightweight 6 mm suits splashbacks and wall cladding, while 12 mm is used for the worktop. Using the same colour gives a very elegant visual continuity, especially with marble effects. Our surveyors plan the slab layout to line up the veins between worktop and splashback as closely as possible.
