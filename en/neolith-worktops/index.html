Inspiration

Why Neolith for your projects

The Size is the Spanish manufacturer of Neolith ceramic. Its slabs reach 3,200 × 1,500 mm, and their slim thickness, 6 or 12 mm, makes the material ideal for cladding a kitchen continuously: worktop, full-height splashback, island fronts or even whole walls.

Like other ceramics, Neolith resists UV, scratches and stains, and needs no treatment. Its reduced thickness gives it a very contemporary look, with slim edges that make units appear lighter.

That slimness has a trade-off: 12 mm used as a worktop must be carried by a perfectly flat, continuous support, to be provided on site. On our side, we check that your dimensions are consistent, carry out CNC cutting and edge finishing, then check every piece before packing it in a way suited to large formats.

Neolith colours: guiding the choice

With more than 60 patterns, Neolith covers every style. Lovers of white marble will look at Calacatta Luxe, Estatuario or Calacatta Gold, the latter two designed for seamless large formats. For a warmer feel, White Sands, a sandy white with beige nuances, or Pietra di Luna bring mineral softness.

More graphic kitchens will find what they need in Basalt Black with its basalt sheen, Nero Zimbabwe, or oxidised metal effects such as Iron Copper and Iron Corten. Amazónico, a white with green veins, adds a rare botanical touch.

Polished, matt and satin Silk finishes are not all available on every colour, and some references only come in 12 mm: check these details in the catalogue below before finalising your drawings.

Your questions

Are The Size and Neolith the same thing?

Yes. The Size is the name of the Spanish manufacturer, and Neolith is the name of its large-format ceramic. On our website the range appears as The Size, but if your client or architect has specified a Neolith worktop, this is it. The colours shown below, such as Calacatta Luxe or Iron Grey, are Neolith references that we fabricate to measure.

Can a Neolith worktop and splashback be matched?

It is one of the range's strong points. The light 6 mm suits splashbacks and wall cladding, while 12 mm is used for the worktop. In the same colour, the visual continuity is very elegant, especially on marble effects. Vein matching between worktop and splashback is prepared from the drawings and slab layout you send us.
