Bespoke wall and floor cladding

Stone cladding turns a wall, a shower or a floor into an architectural feature in its own right. We make, from your drawings:

  • wall cladding for kitchens and bathrooms, including full-height splashbacks;
  • shower walls and trays, carved from the solid or in large slabs;
  • floor tiles in the formats set by your layout;
  • linings for niches, door surrounds and window reveals;
  • island ends and returns matching the worktop.

Large-format ceramics cover wide areas with very few joints, while marble, quartzite or granite bring the depth of natural stone. Each piece is CNC cut to your dimensions, with the cut-outs needed for sockets, switches, taps or drains, then finished by hand on the visible edges.

Architectural and decorative pieces

Beyond cladding, we make the pieces that define an interior architecture project or a venue open to the public:

  • fireplace surrounds, lintels and hearths;
  • reception, bar and shop counters;
  • stone tables, tops and desks;
  • sills, thresholds, copings and window boards;
  • decorative or furniture pieces designed by the architect.

These pieces call for a careful reading of the drawings and early technical dialogue. We study thicknesses, joints, edges and weight and handling constraints with you, so that we can offer a realistic manufacturing approach without betraying the design.

We draw on all our materials for this, from natural stone to Corian®, which allows curved shapes and joints with no visible seam, as well as quartz, ceramic, Minéralis and recycled stone.

Layout planning, inspection and delivery

With cladding, the result depends as much on the layout as on the cutting. From your drawings and technical files, for example DXF, DWG or dimensioned PDF, we plan how the pieces are distributed across the slabs to limit offcuts, place the joints in the right spots and carry the veining from one piece to the next where the slab allows.

For a natural stone with strong veining, we invite you to approve the slabs at our Igreja Nova factory, by appointment, with your client if needed.

Each piece is then inspected for dimensions, cut-outs and appearance, securely packed and labelled according to your layout so it is easy to identify on site. We deliver to France, Portugal and internationally. On-site surveying and setting in place remain your responsibility or that of your partners.

Your questions

Can you keep the veining continuous across several pieces?

When the slab and dimensions allow, yes. We plan the layout to carry the veining from a worktop to its splashback, from one wall to another or across an island end. The result depends on the format of the slabs available and the pattern of the stone. We tell you at the study stage what can be achieved, and a factory visit lets you approve the slabs.

Which material is best for a large wall cladding?

Large-format ceramics, such as Dekton®, The Size, Infinity or MStone, cover large areas with few joints and reduced weight thanks to their slim thicknesses. Natural stone brings unrivalled depth, but its slabs are heavier and more limited in size. We help you decide based on the look, the substrate and handling constraints.

Do you put the cladding in place?

No. CB Prod works exclusively on bespoke manufacturing and delivery, with no on-site measuring or setting in place. We deliver inspected pieces, labelled according to your layout and ready to be put in place by your teams or partners. We remain available to answer their technical questions about the material.
