Integrated Corian® basins: a seamless top

DuPont™ Corian® is consistent throughout its thickness, so basin and top can form one continuous unit, with no visible joint or crevice where water and limescale collect. Its strengths for your bathroom projects:

  • curved shapes and slim edges thanks to thermoforming;
  • a repairable surface, where a scratch can be polished out;
  • more than 100 colours, in 6, 12 or 19 mm;
  • easy cleaning, with one sweep of a sponge towards the waste.

Corian® is more sensitive to heat and scratches than stone or ceramic. In a bathroom, this is rarely an issue. For a double vanity top, both basins can be integrated into a single seamless top, whether for a hotel room or a high-end residence.

Which materials for a bespoke vanity top?

Water, soap, limescale and cosmetics are the real enemies of a vanity top. Each family has its own answer:

  • quartz, ceramic and Minéralis, non-porous and needing no sealing, for heavy use;
  • marble and travertine, for a bathroom with character, provided they are sealed and cleaned with gentle products;
  • marble-effect ceramic, for the look of stone without its constraints;
  • Corian®, for integrated basins and organic shapes.

The choice also depends on the type of basin. An inset or undermount basin works with every material. An integrated basin points towards Corian®, while a basin carved from the solid is studied case by case, depending on the stone and its thickness. We review with you the right solution for your drawings and the planned vanity unit.

Splashbacks, cladding and your technical file

For a coherent bathroom, the vanity top material can continue up the wall. A large-format splashback with no joints protects the wall from splashes and is easier to clean than tiles. 12 mm ceramics, such as MStone's, are particularly well suited to walls. We also make matching shower cladding, niches and shelves, depending on the chosen material.

To quote and manufacture without errors, send us:

  • a dimensioned drawing of each element, for example in DXF, DWG or PDF;
  • the basin and tap references, with their centre distances;
  • the position of wastes and holes;
  • the material, thickness, finish and edge profile.

Every piece is checked before dispatch, then securely packed and delivered across France, Portugal and internationally.

Your questions

Which material should I specify for hotel or residential vanity tops?

For heavy use and minimal upkeep, choose a non-porous surface: quartz, ceramic, Minéralis or Corian®. Corian® is the obvious choice if you want a seamless integrated basin that can be repaired if scratched. Marble and travertine have unrivalled charm, but need sealing and gentle cleaning products.

Can you make one-piece double vanity tops?

Yes. We make one-piece double vanity tops in natural stone, ceramic or Corian®, with inset, undermount or integrated basins, within the limits of the slab format. Cut-outs are set out from the tap and waste positions shown on your drawings. Just specify the vanity unit width and the basin models.

Do you work on projects with multiple bathrooms?

Yes, we manufacture for architects, interior contractors and building firms, including runs of identical or varied elements. Send us the drawings for each bathroom type with the quantities: we study how pieces are cut from the slabs to reduce offcuts and send you a quote within 48 working hours.
