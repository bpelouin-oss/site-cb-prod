Vanity tops tailored to your bathroom

Bathroom vanity tops made to measure fit your cabinet, walls and taps precisely. Single or double basin, countertop or seamlessly integrated: we make yours in our workshop in Mafra, Portugal, in the material that suits the way you use the room and your style.

Integrated Corian® basins: a seamless top

DuPont™ Corian® is uniform through its entire thickness. The basin and the top can therefore form one continuous piece, with no visible joint and no corners where water and limescale can build up. Its strengths in a bathroom:

  • curves and slim edges, thanks to thermoforming;
  • a repairable surface, where a scratch can be polished out;
  • more than 100 colours, in 6, 12 or 19 mm;
  • easy cleaning, with a single wipe towards the plughole.

Corian® is more sensitive to heat and scratches than stone or ceramic. In a bathroom this rarely matters, but avoid resting hair straighteners on it while they are still hot. For a double vanity, both basins can be integrated into a single continuous top.

A vanity top that copes with water every day

Water, soap, limescale and cosmetics are the real enemies of a vanity top. Non-porous surfaces are the most worry-free: quartz, ceramic, Minéralis and Corian® need no sealing and do not hold on to make-up or toothpaste.

Natural stone is still a beautiful choice, provided it is cared for. Marble and travertine, stunning in a bathroom, are sealed and dislike acidic products and harsh descalers; a mild cleaner is all you need day to day.

Craftsmanship matters as much as the material. Basin cut-outs and tap holes are CNC-machined and then finished by hand, and the joins with the wall are carefully sealed during installation so that water cannot seep behind the cabinet.

Which materials for bathroom vanity tops?

Each family brings its own style:

  • marble and travertine, for a bathroom with character, like our double vanity tops in travertine;
  • ceramic, maintenance-free and available in marble effects, such as the Calacatta Oro set on a light wood cabinet in one of our projects;
  • quartz and Minéralis, for a consistent look and a surface that is easy to live with as a family;
  • Corian®, for integrated basins and free-form shapes.

The choice also depends on the type of basin. A countertop or inset basin works with every material. An integrated basin points towards Corian®, while a basin carved from the solid is assessed case by case, depending on the stone and its thickness. We will advise you based on your cabinet and your taps.

Matching splashbacks and shower cladding

For a coherent bathroom, the vanity top material can continue up the wall. A splashback behind the basins protects the wall from splashes and is much easier to clean than tiles, as there are no grout lines. 12 mm ceramics, such as MStone's, are particularly well suited to wall installation.

You can go further and clad the shower in the same stone. We have created a shower clad in Calacatta marble against black walls, and another in travertine for a warmer feel. A matching shower tray or niches can be considered depending on the material. Your questions

What is the best material for a bathroom vanity top?

For minimal upkeep, go for a non-porous surface: quartz, ceramic, Minéralis or Corian®. Corian® is ideal if you want a seamless integrated basin that forms one piece with the top. Marble and travertine have unrivalled charm, but need sealing and gentle cleaners to keep them looking their best.

Can you make a bespoke double vanity top?

Yes. We make one-piece double vanity tops in natural stone, ceramic or Corian®, with countertop, inset or integrated basins. The cut-outs are aligned with the position of your taps and wastes. Simply include the width of the cabinet and the basin models you have chosen in your request.

Does a Corian® vanity top scratch easily?

Corian® is softer than stone or ceramic and can scratch with use. Its advantage is that it is uniform throughout: surface scratches can be sanded and polished out, restoring its original appearance. In a bathroom, where it takes few knocks, it lasts a long time with simple care.
