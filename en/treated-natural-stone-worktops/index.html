What is treated natural stone?

This is genuine quarried stone, whether granite, quartzite or marble, that receives a surface treatment developed by Cosentino in the factory. The pattern, crystals and hardness of the stone remain those of nature: only its ability to absorb liquids is greatly reduced.

There are two approaches. Sensa is an invisible anti-stain protection that keeps the stone's natural look and removes the need for regular sealing, with the Sensa Protect version on quartzites. Texta pairs the stone with a distinctive feel: a velvety matt on granite, a textured, grainy touch on quartzite, with fingerprints far less visible.

You keep a one-off natural piece, with upkeep close to that of an engineered surface.

Sensa or Texta, granite, quartzite or marble: comparing the ranges

  • Sensa granite: polished or honed granite with SenSa technology, Mohs hardness 6 to 7, in 20 or 30 mm. The most versatile choice for a kitchen.
  • Texta granite: the toughness of granite with a velvety matt finish, soft to the touch and less prone to fingerprints.
  • Sensa quartzite: premium quartzites protected by Sensa Protect, which cope with hot pans, resist UV and scratch less than granite.
  • Texta quartzite: quartzites with bold colours and a textured surface that diffuses light and hides water marks.
  • Texta marble: an exclusive selection of three fine-grained marbles with improved stain resistance. Marble does, however, remain sensitive to acids.

Treated stone: strengths and limits

The first benefit is peace of mind. In our selection guide, treated granite clearly gains in stain resistance and ease of care compared with untreated granite, while keeping its resistance to heat and scratches. You no longer have to watch for water that stops beading or plan a resealing session.

There are still points to bear in mind. The treatment protects the surface; it does not change the nature of the stone: a Texta marble is still marble, softer and sensitive to lemon or vinegar. The choice of colours is also narrower than in our untreated stone ranges. Finally, as with all natural stone, every slab is unique and may differ from the sample.

Your questions

Does Sensa treated granite still need sealing?

No, that is the whole point of SenSa technology: the anti-stain treatment is applied in the factory by Cosentino and spares you the regular sealing that ordinary granite requires. Day-to-day care is simply water and a mild soap. Just avoid very harsh or abrasive products, which are not suitable for any natural stone anyway.

Does the anti-stain treatment change how the stone looks?

With Sensa, the treatment is designed to be invisible: the stone keeps its colours, crystals and original polished or honed finish. With Texta, the look changes on purpose, as it is a finish in its own right: velvety matt on granite, textured on quartzite. If you are unsure, ask us for samples of both finishes to compare at home.

Treated granite or quartz: which is better for a family kitchen?

Both are easy to look after. Quartz offers a perfectly even colour and a non-porous surface. Treated granite gives you real, one-off stone and better heat resistance, which matters if you often put pans down near the hob. If you love natural patterns and cook a lot, treated granite is often the best compromise.
