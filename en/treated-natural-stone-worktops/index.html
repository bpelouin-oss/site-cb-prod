What is stain-resistant treated granite?

It is genuine quarried stone, whether granite, quartzite or marble, which receives a surface treatment developed by Cosentino at the factory. The pattern, crystals and hardness of the stone remain those of nature: only its ability to absorb liquids is greatly reduced.

Two approaches exist. Sensa is an invisible stain protection that preserves the natural look and removes the need for regular sealing, with the Sensa Protect version on quartzites. Texta pairs the stone with a finish that has a distinctive feel: velvety matt on granite, textured on quartzite, with fingerprints less visible.

Your client keeps a one-off piece, with upkeep close to that of an engineered surface.

Sensa or Texta, granite, quartzite or marble: the ranges we fabricate

  • Sensa granite: polished or honed granite with SenSa technology, Mohs hardness 6 to 7, in 20 or 30 mm. The most versatile choice for a kitchen.
  • Texta granite: the toughness of granite with a velvety matt finish, soft to the touch and less prone to fingerprints.
  • Sensa quartzite: premium quartzites protected by Sensa Protect, which take hot pans, resist UV and scratch less than granite.
  • Texta quartzite: boldly coloured quartzites with a textured surface that diffuses light and hides water marks.
  • Texta marble: a selection of three fine-grained marbles with improved stain resistance. Marble nevertheless remains sensitive to acids.

We source from Cosentino according to the range and colour stated on your drawings.

Strengths and limits of treated stone

The first advantage is peace of mind for the end user. In our selection guide, treated granite gains significantly in stain resistance and ease of upkeep compared with untreated granite, while keeping its resistance to heat and scratches. There is no longer any need to watch for water that no longer beads or to schedule resealing.

Some points should still be made clear to the client. The treatment protects the surface; it does not change the nature of the stone: a Texta marble is still marble, softer and sensitive to lemon or vinegar. The choice of colours is narrower than in our untreated stone ranges. Finally, like all natural stone, each slab is unique and may differ from the sample: a factory visit by appointment lets you see the slabs.

Your questions

Does Sensa-treated granite still need sealing?

No, that is the whole point of SenSa technology: the stain-resistant treatment is applied at the factory by Cosentino and removes the need for the regular sealing that standard granite requires. The upkeep you can describe to your client comes down to water and a mild soap, avoiding very harsh or abrasive products, which suit no natural stone anyway.

Does the treatment change the look of the stone?

With Sensa, the treatment is designed to be invisible: the stone keeps its colours, crystals and original polished or honed finish. With Texta, the look changes deliberately, as it is a finish in its own right: matt and velvety on granite, textured on quartzite. To compare the two, the safest approach is to see them side by side, as samples or full slabs.

Treated granite or quartz: which to specify for a family kitchen?

Both are easy to look after. Quartz offers perfectly consistent colour and a non-porous surface, useful across several identical homes. Treated granite brings genuine, one-off stone and better heat resistance, which matters if your client cooks a lot near the hob. We fabricate both at our Mafra factory.
