Matching the material to the use

To advise your clients, start from how the room will really be used rather than from the colour. A hard-working kitchen worktop does not call for the same material as a guest bathroom vanity top or a statement dining table.

Four criteria make the difference:

  • resistance to scratches and knocks;
  • heat tolerance near the cooking area;
  • sensitivity to stains: wine, coffee, oil, lemon;
  • maintenance, with or without sealing.

Our comparison chart rates each family on these points. It is only a guide: thickness and finish also change how a surface behaves, and a high polish shows marks more readily than a honed or leather finish. To make the final call, you are welcome to see slabs and samples at our factory in Igreja Nova, Monday to Friday, by appointment.

Natural stone or engineered surfaces?

Natural stone (granite, marble, quartzite) comes straight from the quarry. Every slab is unique, with veining that is never repeated. Granite and quartzite, with a Mohs hardness of 6 to 7, stand up well to heat and scratches. Marble, softer and sensitive to acids, needs sealing and is at its best in bathrooms and high-end statement projects.

Engineered surfaces work differently. Quartz (Silestone®, Compac, Stone Italiana, MStone) is non-porous and very easy to look after. Ceramic (Dekton®, The Size, Infinity, MStone) scores highest for resistance to scratches, heat and stains. Corian® stands out for its integrated basins and repairable surface. Between the two worlds, Sensa and Texta treated granites and quartzites keep the look of natural stone with fewer constraints.

More responsible materials for your projects

Several ranges in our catalogue reduce a project's footprint without compromising everyday performance, which gives you one more argument with your clients and developers.

  • Minéralis, distributed by MStone Surfaces: 85% first-production recycled glass and a low silica content, safer for the people who cut and work the material.
  • MStone recycled stone: recycled minerals bound with plant-based eco-resins, for a carbon footprint up to 40% lower.
  • Recycled Compac: fragments of quartz and marble, suited to projects targeting HQE, BREEAM or LEED.
  • Ostrea: crushed oyster and scallop shells with a pearly sheen.

A responsible choice is also about lifespan: a surface chosen for the way the room is really used will not need replacing any time soon.

From raw material to delivery

CB Prod buys its materials from international suppliers and selects slabs to suit your projects. Drawing on the group's family expertise, handed down over more than three generations, our Mafra workshop then takes over.

From the plans and technical files you send us, for example DXF, DWG or dimensioned PDF, we study each piece and optimise how it is cut from the slab. Pieces are cut on CNC machines, then edges and cut-outs are finished by hand. Before dispatch, we check dimensions, finishes and conformity with your drawings.

The pieces are then securely packed and delivered across France, Portugal and internationally. Site surveys and the work on site remain with you or your partners.

Your questions

Which material should I recommend for a hard-working worktop?

In our comparison chart, ceramic (Dekton®, The Size, Infinity, MStone) earns top marks for scratches, heat, stains and maintenance. Quartzite and granite follow closely on hardness and heat, but are slightly more prone to staining. The right choice also depends on the look your client wants and on the room in question.

Can you source a specific material for a project?

Yes, sourcing is part of what we do: we select and buy materials from international suppliers. In your enquiry, state the range, colour, thickness and finish you need. We check slab availability and, if needed, suggest an equivalent reference from our catalogue.

How do I get a quote for several materials?

The price depends on the material, range, thickness, cut-outs and finishes, so we do not publish standard rates. Send us your dimensioned plans or technical files, listing the materials you are considering. We reply within 48 working hours and can price two or three options to help you present the project to your client.
