Your questions about our bespoke worktops

How soon will I receive my quote?

Every request is reviewed by an expert, who will send you a tailored proposal within 48 working hours. If any information is missing (dimensions, cut-outs, finish), they will contact you before preparing an accurate price.

Do you deliver and install in France?

Yes. We manufacture in our factory in Mafra, Portugal, then deliver and install in both France and Portugal with our own teams. Supply only is also available if you have your own fitter.

Can I see the materials before choosing?

Of course. Come and see the slabs and samples for real at our factory in Igreja Nova (Mafra), Monday to Friday from 8:00 am to 5:30 pm, by appointment only.

What is the difference between quartz and quartzite?

Quartzite is a natural stone extracted from quarries: Mohs hardness 7, veining similar to marble and better resistance to acids and stains than marble.

Quartz (Silestone®, Compac, Stone Italiana) is an engineered, quartz-based surface: non-porous, uniform, requires no sealing and is very easy to maintain.

What is Minéralis?

Minéralis, distributed by MStone Surfaces, is a composite mineral surface that replaces quartz: 85% first-production recycled glass, a low silica content, heat resistance up to 180 °C and a 20-year warranty. It comes in 19 colours, which we craft to measure in Mafra.

Is marble suitable for a kitchen?

Marble is sublime but softer (Mohs hardness 3–4) and sensitive to acids: it needs sealing and a little care in the kitchen. It truly excels in bathrooms, as a table or as a statement piece.

For a hard-working kitchen with a marble look, we would steer you towards quartzite, ceramic or a veined quartz.

How does the survey work?

Our surveyors visit your home to record every cut-out, angle and hob opening precisely. Manufacturing begins on the basis of this survey, which guarantees a perfect fit on installation.

What warranties apply to the materials?

We work with brands that stand behind their surfaces: up to 25 years depending on the range, and 20 years on Minéralis. On installation, we give you care advice tailored to your material.

Do you work with trade professionals?

Yes. Kitchen specialists, architects, craftsmen and distributors benefit from preferential pricing, an extended catalogue and a dedicated contact. Visit the trade area to join our network.
