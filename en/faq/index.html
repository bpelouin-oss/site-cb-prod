Your questions about bespoke manufacturing at CB Prod

Where is your factory?

Our factory is in Igreja Nova, Mafra, north of Lisbon, Portugal. This is where we process and shape natural stone and technical surfaces for our professional clients, before delivering to France, Portugal and worldwide.

Do you work with French kitchen specialists and interior fitters?

Yes. We manufacture for the CB Surfaces group companies (CB Surfaces, Easy Plan, Kitchenfab) and for independent professionals in France and Portugal: kitchen specialists, stonemasons, architects, interior fitters, distributors and specialist companies. Each client benefits from terms adapted to their volumes and a dedicated contact.

Do you take site measurements and install?

No. CB Prod works exclusively on bespoke fabrication and delivery. We manufacture from the drawings, dimensions and technical information you provide; site measurement and installation remain the responsibility of you or your partners.

Our technical team reviews your drawings and files before production and flags anything that needs checking.

How do I request a manufacturing quote?

Fill in the three-step quote form: type of pieces, material, dimensions and delivery location. You immediately receive an acknowledgement by email: reply to it to send us your drawings, dimensions and technical files. The quote is free.

How soon will I receive my quote?

Every request is reviewed by an expert, who will send you a tailored proposal within 48 working hours. If any information is missing (dimensions, cut-outs, finish), they will contact you before preparing an accurate price.

Do you deliver to France and abroad?

Yes. We manufacture in our factory in Mafra, Portugal, then deliver to France, Portugal and worldwide. CB Prod does not install: installation remains with you or your partners.

Which materials do you work with?

Natural stone (marble, granite, quartzite), quartz, large-format ceramic, Corian® and Minéralis, sourced from the leading material brands. All families and colours are presented in our materials pages.

Can I see the materials before choosing?

Of course. Come and see the slabs and samples for real at our factory in Igreja Nova (Mafra), Monday to Friday from 8:00 am to 5:30 pm, by appointment only.

What is the difference between quartz and quartzite?

Quartzite is a natural stone extracted from quarries: Mohs hardness 7, veining similar to marble and better resistance to acids and stains than marble.

Quartz (Silestone®, Compac, Stone Italiana) is an engineered, quartz-based surface: non-porous, uniform, requires no sealing and is very easy to maintain.

What is Minéralis?

Minéralis, distributed by MStone Surfaces, is a composite mineral surface that replaces quartz: 85% first-production recycled glass, a low silica content, heat resistance up to 180 °C and a 20-year warranty. It comes in 19 colours, which we craft to measure in Mafra.

Is marble suitable for a kitchen?

Marble is sublime but softer (Mohs hardness 3–4) and sensitive to acids: it needs sealing and a little care in the kitchen. It truly excels in bathrooms, as a table or as a statement piece.

For a hard-working kitchen with a marble look, we would steer you towards quartzite, ceramic or a veined quartz.

What warranties apply to the materials?

We work with brands that guarantee their surfaces: up to 25 years depending on the range, and 20 years on Minéralis. We provide the technical documentation and care advice for each material.
