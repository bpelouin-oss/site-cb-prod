Inspiration

Why specify Sensa granite

Sensa granite suits a specific end client: someone who wants a natural stone that copes with everyday cooking, without watching over the worktop or getting out the sealer every year. SenSa protection is invisible: it creates no glossy film and no change in colour.

The stone keeps its Mohs hardness of 6 to 7, its heat resistance and its scratch resistance. Splashes of oil, coffee or wine, which would mark an untreated light granite, wipe away far more easily. The treatment is covered by a manufacturer's warranty.

Sensa granite comes polished or honed, in 20 or 30 mm. We fabricate it like standard granite: CNC cutting, sink and hob cut-outs, edges and hand finishing, all to your drawings.

Sensa granite colours: guiding the choice

It is on light granites that Sensa protection adds the most. Colonial White, Delicatus, a delicate white with gold veins, or Bianco Romano, with grey and pink veins, give a bright kitchen without fear of the first oil stain. Arctic Cream and Giallo Ornamental play on cream and golden tones, warm alongside wood.

Fans of darker shades will find Black Beauty, Indian Black or Moak Black, elegant and easy to live with. For a touch of colour, Verde Bahia offers a bright tropical green, and Itara a golden brown.

Like any natural stone, each slab differs slightly: a factory visit by appointment lets you see the slabs. Some colours only come polished: the catalogue below shows the finishes available for each.

Your questions

Does the Sensa treatment wear off over time?

The SenSa treatment is applied at the factory by Cosentino and is covered by a manufacturer's warranty, whose terms are set by Cosentino. To preserve it, the user cleans with water and mild soap and avoids strippers, strong solvents and abrasive pads. No sealer needs to be applied on top: simple guidance to pass on to your client.

Is Sensa granite available in a matt finish?

Yes. Depending on the colour, Sensa granite comes polished and honed, or in glossy Mir and the matt Suède and Cas finishes. Only Verde Bahia is available polished only. Matt gives a more natural, contemporary look with fewer reflections. For an even softer matt feel, Cosentino's Texta granite is another option to present.
