Inspiration

Why choose Sensa granite for your worktop

Sensa granite is for a specific kind of client: you want a natural stone that can handle everyday cooking, but you do not want to keep an eye on your worktop or bring out the sealant every year. The SenSa protection is invisible: it creates no glossy film and no change of colour.

The stone keeps its Mohs hardness of 6 to 7, its heat tolerance and its scratch resistance. Splashes of oil, coffee or wine, which would mark an untreated pale granite, wipe away much more easily. A manufacturer's warranty covers the treatment.

Sensa granite comes polished or honed, in 20 or 30 mm. It is often the best choice for a family that loves the charm of real stone.

Choosing your Sensa granite colour

Sensa protection makes the biggest difference on pale granites. Colonial White, Delicatus, a delicate white with golden veins, or Bianco Romano, with grey and pink veins, give you a bright kitchen without dreading the first oil stain. Arctic Cream and Giallo Ornamental bring cream and golden tones, warm alongside wood.

Lovers of dark shades will find Black Beauty, Indian Black or Moak Black, elegant and easy to live with. For a touch of colour, Verde Bahia offers a bright tropical green, and Itara a golden brown.

As with all natural stone, each slab differs slightly. Some colours only come polished: the catalogue below lists the finishes available for each one.

Your questions

Does the Sensa treatment wear off over time?

The SenSa treatment is applied in the factory by Cosentino and is covered by a manufacturer's warranty, the terms of which we give you with your quote. To preserve it, clean with water and mild soap and avoid strippers, strong solvents and abrasive pads. You do not need to apply any sealant on top.

Is Sensa granite available in a matt finish?

Yes. Depending on the colour, Sensa granite comes polished and honed, or in glossy Mir and the matt Suède and Cas finishes. Only Verde Bahia is offered polished only. Matt gives a more natural, contemporary look with fewer reflections. If you want a matt that is even softer to the touch, also take a look at Cosentino's Texta granite, whose velvety finish is a signature in its own right.
